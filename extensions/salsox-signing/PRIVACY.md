# Privacy: Salsox Signing

**Last updated:** 25 October 2026

Salsox Signing is a client for your Salsox account. It sends what you prepare in it to Salsox, and to no one else.

- **What it sends, and where.** Only to salsox.com: the PDF you choose, the signers' email addresses and names you type, the fields you place, the title and message. This is the same information as sending a document from salsox.com, and Salsox's privacy policy (https://salsox.com/privacy) covers it there.
- **What it stores on your computer.** In the browser's extension storage: the API key it was given when you connected, your account's email, name and plan, and the document you are preparing until it is sent. Disconnecting deletes all of it and revokes the key; removing the extension deletes it too.
- **What it reads.** Only the PDF you choose. It does not read the pages you browse, your tabs or any other site.
- **What it does not do.** No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no remote code. It contacts no server but salsox.com.
