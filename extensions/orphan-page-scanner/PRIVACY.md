# Privacy: Orphan Page Scanner by Salsox

**Last updated:** 24 October 2026

The Orphan Page Scanner collects no personal data and sends nothing to Salsox or to anyone else.

- **What it reads.** Only the website you choose to scan: its `robots.txt`, its sitemap and the pages the sitemap lists. Requests are made from your browser, without your cookies, directly to that website.
- **What it stores.** The last result for each site you scanned (the page addresses, their link counts and status), in your browser's extension storage on your computer. Removing the extension removes it.
- **What it shares.** Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no account and no remote code.
- **Permissions.** Access to a site is requested only when you press Scan on it, and only for that site.
