# Launch day: Tuesday 3 November 2026

Product Hunt's day runs from **12:01 AM to 11:59 PM Pacific**. US daylight
saving ends on 1 November, so that week Pacific is UTC−8:
**launch at 8:01 AM Accra time, Tuesday**, and the day ends at **7:59 AM Accra
time, Wednesday**. All times below are Accra (GMT).

## The day before (Monday)

- [ ] The launch is scheduled in Product Hunt for 3 Nov, 12:01 AM PST, with
      the tagline, description, topics, links, thumbnail and the five gallery
      images in order (listing.md).
- [ ] The maker comment is saved somewhere you can paste from your phone.
- [ ] Sign up on salsox.com with a fresh email, Quick Send a PDF to yourself,
      and sign it on your phone. Fix anything that breaks tonight, not tomorrow.
- [ ] If you are offering a promo code, test it at checkout (listing.md, Pricing).
- [ ] Draft the X and LinkedIn posts (below), with the Product Hunt link left blank.

## Avoid on Product Hunt

- **Asking for upvotes.** Never say "upvote" or "support us", and never link
  straight to the vote button. Ask for feedback instead. Product Hunt penalises
  vote-begging, and it reads badly.
- **Vote swaps, paid votes, launch groups that trade votes, or many accounts.**
  Product Hunt detects them, and the launch can be removed.
- **Mass DMs, or the same message pasted to strangers.** Write to people who know you, one at a time.
- **Brand-new accounts voting for you.** Their votes count for little, and a spike of them looks like manipulation.
- **Claims you cannot back.** No user numbers, customers, reviews or "#1" until they are real.
- **Arguing in the comments.** Thank critics, answer the point, and fix what is fair.
- **Editing the listing all day.** Get it right the night before; small fixes only.

## After launch (Wednesday)

- Reply to any comments that came in overnight.
- Write down every feature request and bug from the thread, and tell the
  people who asked when you ship it.
