## How it is built

- **Cadence.** LinkedIn: one post each weekday (10). X: one post each weekday plus two threads (12). YouTube: one long video a week plus two Shorts a week (6).
- **Themes by day.** Monday, a problem people recognise. Tuesday, a how-to. Wednesday, a feature shown in use. Thursday, proof or a comparison. Friday, something useful and free.
- **Facts only.** Every claim below is something the product does today. Things that are planned (a Zapier app, a public API) are described as planned, never as available. No user counts or revenue figures: we have none we can stand behind publicly yet.
- **Links.** Each link carries `utm_source` (linkedin, x or youtube), `utm_medium=social` and `utm_campaign=cal_2026_10`, so signups show up by channel on the admin SEO page. Written below as `[link: /path]`; add the tags when scheduling, or let the social studio do it.
- **Hashtags.** X: at most two. LinkedIn: three at the end. Same sets the social studio suggests.
- **Times.** LinkedIn 8:30, X 12:00, YouTube 15:00 (US Eastern), for a US, Canada and UK audience. Adjust after week one's numbers.
- **Three posts have a prerequisite:** 3 and 7 November need the e-signature law country pages published (they were not live on 24 October), and 5 November needs the Chrome extension live in the Chrome Web Store.

### Tuesday 3 November

**LinkedIn**

> "Is an electronic signature legally binding?"
>
> In the US, Canada, the UK and Germany, the short answer is yes, for most business documents. The longer answer is in the law itself, and it matters:
>
> - US: the federal E-SIGN Act and state UETA laws.
> - Canada: PIPEDA Part 2 federally, and each province's electronic commerce act.
> - UK: the Electronic Communications Act 2000 and UK eIDAS.
> - Germany: the EU eIDAS Regulation, with the German Civil Code still requiring written or notarised form for some documents.
>
> Each country also excludes certain documents, like wills and some property transfers.
>
> We wrote one page per country, quoting the actual sections, with the date we checked them. Not legal advice; a starting point you can verify.
>
> [link: /esignature-law]
>
> #esignature #legal #compliance
