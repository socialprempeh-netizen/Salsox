This policy explains what personal data Salsox collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It covers our website, the Salsox app, and the signing pages that people receive documents through.

If you have a question about it, write to [contact@salsox.com](mailto:contact@salsox.com).

## Who we are

Salsox is an e-signature service: senders upload documents, choose who signs them, and can ask for a payment as part of signing ("Sign & Pay"). In this policy, "Salsox", "we" and "us" mean the operator of the Salsox service.

We play two different roles:

- **For your own account** (if you sign up to send documents), we decide how your data is used. We are the controller of that data.
- **For the documents you send**, and the details of the people you send them to, we process that data on your behalf, to carry out the signing you asked for. You decide what goes into a document and who receives it.

## What we collect

**When you create an account:** your name, your email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash), or the name, email and profile picture your Google or GitHub account shares with us if you sign in that way. If you turn on two-factor authentication, we store the secret it needs.

**When you use Salsox:** the documents you upload, the people you add to them (their names, email addresses and, if you choose, phone numbers), the fields you place, and the messages you write. We record an audit trail for every document: what happened, when, and from which IP address and browser.

**When someone signs a document:** what they enter in each field (including drawn, typed or uploaded signatures), the time they viewed and signed, their IP address and browser, and, if they decline, the reason they give. These become part of the document's audit trail, which is what makes a signature verifiable.

**When you pay us:** payments for your plan are handled by Stripe. We receive a customer reference, your plan, the status of your subscription and your invoices. We never see or store your full card number.

**When someone pays through Sign & Pay:** the payment is handled by Stripe or Paystack, and the money goes to the sender's own payout account. We receive the payment's status, amount, currency and reference, and the payer's email address. We never see or store card numbers or mobile money PINs. If a sender connects a payout account, the provider collects the identity and bank details it requires; we keep only a reference to that account and whether it is ready.

**When you contact us or join our newsletter:** your email address, your message, and, for the newsletter, when you confirmed your subscription.

**Automatically:** our hosting provider records technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the service running and secure. We use privacy-friendly analytics that count page views without cookies and without building a profile of you.

## Why we use it

- **To provide the service:** to send documents, collect signatures and payments, seal signed copies, and send the emails that go with them (invitations, reminders, receipts, signed copies).
- **To keep a reliable record:** the audit trail exists so that a signature can be checked later. That is a core part of what an e-signature service does.
- **To keep accounts and the service secure:** to confirm email addresses, prevent spam and phishing sent through Salsox, enforce sending limits, detect abuse, and investigate disputes.
- **To bill you:** to manage your plan, send invoices and remind you before a renewal.
- **To meet legal obligations:** for example tax and accounting records.
- **To improve Salsox:** using aggregated, non-identifying statistics.

Where a law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running a secure and reliable service (for audit trails and abuse prevention); legal obligations; and, for the newsletter, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

We use these service providers to run Salsox, each bound to use the data only to provide their service to us:

- **Vercel**, for hosting, file storage (your documents) and cookie-free analytics.
- **Our database provider**, which stores account and document records.
- **Resend**, to send email.
- **Stripe** and **Paystack**, to process payments for plans and for Sign & Pay.
- **A rate-limiting service**, which may process IP addresses briefly to stop abuse.

The people you send a document to will see your name or email as the sender, the document, and, once it is completed, the other signers' names and the audit trail on the signed copy.

We may disclose data when the law requires it, to protect people from harm or fraud, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

Some of these providers process data outside the country you live in. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the provider's standard contractual protections.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account** is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account (from Settings) deletes your documents, their signed copies, their audit trails and your payout account references.
- **Documents and audit trails** are kept for as long as the sender keeps their account, because a signed document is only useful if its evidence is still there. Senders can export everything at any time.
- **Billing records** may be kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after you delete your account.
- **Logs** kept by our hosting provider are retained for a limited period and then deleted.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format, object to or restrict certain uses, and withdraw consent. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

- Senders can see, export and delete their own data from the app.
- If you signed a document someone sent you, the sender controls that document. Ask them first; if you cannot reach them, write to us and we will help.

To exercise a right, write to [contact@salsox.com](mailto:contact@salsox.com). We will answer within the time the law allows, usually one month.

## Security

We use encryption in transit, store passwords only as one-way hashes, keep documents in private storage reachable only through checked links, and record a fingerprint of every original and signed file so that any change can be detected. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## Children

Salsox is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell account holders by email before the change takes effect.
