Salsox uses as little browser storage as it can. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

## Cookies we set

| Cookie | What it is for | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `better-auth.session_token` | Keeps you signed in. Without it you would have to sign in on every page. | Until you sign out, or the session expires |
| `better-auth.session_data` | A short-lived copy of your session, so pages load faster. | About one minute |

On a secure connection these names may start with `__Secure-`. They are only set when you sign in to an account; people who just read our website or sign a document they were sent do not get them.

These cookies are strictly necessary for the service to work, so they do not need your consent.

## Other storage in your browser

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `theme` (local storage) | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. |
| `sidebar-collapsed` (local storage) | Remembers whether you collapsed the dashboard sidebar. |

This stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## Analytics

We count page views with Vercel Analytics, which works without cookies and without identifying you.

## Payment pages

When you pay, through a plan checkout or Sign & Pay, you are taken to a page run by Stripe or Paystack. Those pages may set their own cookies, for example to prevent fraud, under the provider's own cookie policy.

## Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookies, you will not be able to stay signed in to Salsox.

## Questions

Write to [contact@salsox.com](mailto:contact@salsox.com). Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains how we handle personal data more generally.
