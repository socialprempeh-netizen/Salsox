about: |
  A bill of sale is the receipt for a private vehicle sale. It proves who sold the vehicle to whom, when, and for how much, and it identifies the vehicle by its VIN so there is no doubt which one changed hands. Licensing offices in many places ask to see one when the buyer registers the vehicle, and it protects the seller if the vehicle is later involved in a fine or an accident after the handover.

  This template records the odometer reading, everything handed over with the vehicle, and any defects the buyer was shown, and states that the vehicle is sold as seen. The seller confirms they own it outright, that nothing is owed on it, and that the mileage is accurate to their knowledge.

  A bill of sale does not transfer the registration on its own. Both parties should still complete the licensing authority's transfer form, and the seller should tell the authority the vehicle has been sold. With {site}, both sign on the spot and leave with the same copy.
