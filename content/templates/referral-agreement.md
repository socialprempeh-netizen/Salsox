## 2. Registration

The Partner registers each referral before the prospect is contacted by the Company. The Company confirms within five working days whether it accepts the registration. If two partners register the same prospect, the earlier accepted registration counts.

## 3. Commission

For each Qualified Referral, the Company pays the Partner [Commission]. Commission is calculated on amounts actually received, excluding taxes and refunds.

## 5. Conduct

The Partner will describe the Company's products accurately, make no promises on its behalf, and follow any brand guidelines the Company provides. The Partner is an independent party, not the Company's agent, and cannot bind the Company.
