# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Agreement date] between **[Discloser legal name]**, of [Discloser address] (the "Discloser"), and **[Recipient legal name]**, of [Recipient address] (the "Recipient").

## 1. Purpose

The Discloser intends to share confidential information with the Recipient so that the Recipient can engage in [Purpose] (the "Purpose"). The Recipient agrees to protect that information as set out below.

## 2. What is confidential

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information the Discloser shares with the Recipient, whether in writing, verbally, visually or electronically, including designs, plans, source code, business and financial information, customer and supplier details, and anything marked or described as confidential.

It does not include information that the Recipient can show is public through no fault of its own, was already known to it without restriction, was lawfully received from someone else without a duty of confidence, or was developed independently.

## 3. The Recipient's duties

The Recipient will:

- use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- keep it secret and not disclose it to any third party without the Discloser's prior written consent;
- limit access to staff or advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent duties, and remain responsible for them;
- protect it with at least reasonable care; and
- tell the Discloser promptly of any unauthorised use or disclosure.

## 4. Disclosure required by law

If the law or a court requires disclosure, the Recipient will give the Discloser prompt written notice, where allowed, and disclose only the minimum required.

## 5. Return of information

When the Purpose ends, or whenever the Discloser asks, the Recipient will return or securely destroy all Confidential Information, including copies and notes, and confirm this in writing.

## 6. Duration and remedies

The Recipient's duties last for [Duration in years] years from the date above, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain secret. The Recipient accepts that a breach could cause harm that money alone cannot fix, and that the Discloser may seek an injunction in addition to any other remedy.

## 7. General

No licence or ownership passes to the Recipient. This Agreement is governed by [Governing law], is the entire agreement on its subject, and may be signed electronically.
