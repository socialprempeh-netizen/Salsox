about: |
  A volunteer agreement sets out what a volunteer can expect from an organisation and what the organisation hopes for in return. It is a statement of good faith, not a contract of employment, and the wording matters: an agreement that reads like a job, with obligations, pay or penalties, can make a volunteer legally an employee or worker, with all the rights that brings.

  This template avoids that. It describes the role and the time it usually takes as hopes rather than obligations, lists the induction and support the organisation provides, repays only actual out-of-pocket expenses, and lets either side end the arrangement at any time. It asks the volunteer to follow the organisation's policies on confidentiality, safeguarding and health and safety, which protect the people the organisation serves.

  For roles with children or vulnerable adults, add the background checks the law requires before the volunteer starts.

# Volunteer Agreement

This agreement is between [Organisation name] (the "Organisation") and [Volunteer name] (the "Volunteer").

## 1. The role

Role: [Volunteer role]
What it involves: [Role description]
Usual time: [Expected time]
Starting: [Start date]
Your contact: [Supervisor]

## 2. What the Organisation will do

- Provide [Training provided] before the first shift.
- Give the Volunteer a named contact for support and questions, and listen to their ideas and concerns.
- Repay [Expenses covered].
- Provide insurance cover for the Volunteer while they carry out agreed activities.
- Treat the Volunteer fairly and in line with our equal opportunities policy.

## 3. What the Organisation hopes the Volunteer will do

- Let the contact above know as early as possible if they cannot attend a shift.
- Follow the Organisation's policies, including those on confidentiality, safeguarding, and health and safety, which are explained at induction.
- Keep private any personal information about the people the Organisation serves.
- Raise any concern about someone's safety with the named contact straight away.

## 4. Status

This agreement is binding in honour only. It is not a contract of employment and does not create an employment relationship. The Volunteer is not paid for their time, and either party may end the arrangement at any time, ideally with notice.

## 5. Photos and stories

The Organisation will ask separately before using the Volunteer's name or image in any publicity.
