about: |
  A termination letter confirms in writing a decision that has already been explained to the employee in person. It should never be the way someone first learns they are losing their job. What it does is give them, and you, a clear record of the decision, the reason, the dates, and what happens next.

  This template states the reason factually and refers to the earlier warnings or process that led to it, sets out notice and how it will be handled, lists what final pay includes and when it arrives, and explains how to appeal. The employee signs only to acknowledge they received the letter, not that they agree with it, and the letter says so.

  Dismissal law differs widely: some countries require a specific procedure, notice periods set by statute, or written reasons within a deadline. # Termination of Employment

[Letter date]

Dear [Employee name],

**Re: Termination of your employment as [Job title] with [Company name]**

## The decision

Further to our meeting on [Meeting date], I am writing to confirm the decision to end your employment. The reason is [Reason for termination].

## Notice and last day

You are entitled to [Notice period] of notice. [Notice arrangement]. Your last working day is [Last working day].

## Final pay

Your final pay will include [Final pay details], less the usual deductions. You will receive your final payslip and any tax documents in the usual way.

## Company property

Please return [Property to return] by your last working day. Access to company systems will end on that day.

## Your right to appeal

If you disagree with this decision, you may appeal in writing by [Appeal deadline], stating the grounds of your appeal, to [Appeal contact]. The appeal will be heard by someone who was not involved in the decision.

## Next steps

If you have questions about your final pay, benefits or references, please contact the HR team.
