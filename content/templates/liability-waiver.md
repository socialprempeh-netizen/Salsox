about: |
  A liability waiver does two jobs. It makes sure participants understand the real risks of an activity before they choose to take part, and it records that they accepted those risks. For gyms, adventure parks, sports clubs and event organisers, it is standard paperwork.

  This template is written to be read, not skimmed. It names the activity and its specific risks in plain language, lists the safety rules the participant agrees to follow, asks for a health declaration for the conditions that matter for this activity, and records an emergency contact. The release covers the ordinary risks of the activity.

  Waivers have limits, and the form says so: in most countries a business cannot exclude liability for its own negligence causing death or injury, and an overreaching waiver may be unenforceable entirely. # Liability Waiver and Release

**Provider:** [Provider name]
**Activity:** [Activity], on [Activity date]
**Participant:** [Participant name]

## 1. Risks

I understand that [Activity] involves risks, including [Risks]. I am taking part voluntarily.

Participant initials: [[initials:participant]]

## 2. Health declaration

I confirm that I am in good health and do not have any of these conditions, or have told staff about them before taking part: [Health conditions]. I will tell staff at once if I feel unwell during the activity.

Participant initials: [[initials:participant]]

## 3. Rules

I will follow the safety rules: [Safety rules]. I understand that staff may stop me taking part if I do not.

## 4. Release

To the extent the law allows, I release [Provider name], its staff and agents from claims for injury, loss or damage arising from the ordinary risks of the activity described above. This release does not apply to death or personal injury caused by the provider's negligence, or to anything else that cannot be excluded by law.

## 5. Medical treatment and emergency contact

I agree to receive first aid, and emergency medical treatment if needed. Emergency contact: [Emergency contact].

## 6. Photos

Photos taken during the activity will not be published without separate consent.
