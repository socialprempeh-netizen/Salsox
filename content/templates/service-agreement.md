  A service agreement is the backbone contract for most B2B relationships: IT support, cleaning, bookkeeping, maintenance, security, training. It describes what the provider does, to what standard, and on what commercial terms, and it is the document both sides return to when something goes wrong.

  This template keeps the essentials and the clauses that matter when they are tested: a precise description of the services, measurable service levels, fees with a rate for extra work, payment terms, an initial term that then continues, a cap on liability tied to fees, data protection for any personal data the provider handles, and termination with notice or immediately for serious breach.

# Service Agreement

This Service Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Provider legal name]**, of [Provider address] (the "Provider"), and **[Client legal name]**, of [Client address] (the "Client").

## 1. Services

The Provider will provide [Services] (the "Services"), with reasonable skill and care and in line with good industry practice.

## 2. Service levels

The Provider will meet these service levels: [Service levels]. The Provider will report monthly on how they were met.

## 3. Term

This Agreement starts on [Start date] and runs for an initial term of [Initial term]. It then continues until either party ends it under clause 8.

## 4. Fees and payment

The Client will pay [Fees]. The Provider invoices monthly in advance and the Client pays within [Payment days] days. Fees may be reviewed once a year with 60 days' notice.

## 5. Client responsibilities

The Client will give the Provider timely access to premises, systems and people the Services need, and a named contact for day-to-day decisions.

## 6. Data protection and confidentiality

Where the Provider handles personal data for the Client, it will do so only on the Client's instructions, keep it secure, and report any breach without delay. Each party keeps the other's confidential information private.

## 7. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to [Liability cap], except for liability that cannot be limited by law.
