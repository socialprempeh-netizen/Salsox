  This template identifies the space and the one vehicle allowed to use it, sets the rent, payment date and a small deposit, and says how access works, including returning the gate remote or key. # Parking Space Rental Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Owner name] (the "Owner") and [Renter name] (the "Renter").

## 1. The space

The Owner rents to the Renter [Space description] (the "Space"), starting on [Start date], for parking this vehicle only: [Vehicle details]. The Renter will tell the Owner before changing the vehicle.

## 2. Rent and deposit

The rent is [Monthly rent] a month, paid on [Payment date]. The Renter pays a deposit of [Deposit], returned within 14 days after the agreement ends, less any unpaid rent or the cost of repairing damage the Renter caused.

## 3. Access

Access is by [Access method]. A lost remote or key is replaced at the Renter's cost.

## 4. Use

The Renter will use the Space only to park the vehicle above, which must be taxed, insured and roadworthy. The Renter will not store goods or fuel in the Space, carry out repairs there other than minor ones such as changing a bulb, sublet it, or obstruct other spaces or access routes.

## 5. Responsibility

The Renter parks at their own risk and keeps their own vehicle insurance. The Owner is not responsible for loss of or damage to the vehicle or its contents, except where caused by the Owner's negligence. The Renter is responsible for damage they cause to the Space or the building, including oil leaks.

## 6. Building rules

The Renter will follow any rules of the building or car park that the Owner has given them.

## 7. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement by giving [Notice period] written notice. The Owner may end it immediately if the Renter is more than 14 days late with rent or seriously breaks section 4. At the end, the Renter will remove the vehicle and return the access device.
