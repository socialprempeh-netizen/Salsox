# Photography Services Contract

This Contract is made on [Agreement date] between **[Photographer name]** (the "Photographer") and **[Client full name]** (the "Client").

## 1. Booking

Shoot: [Shoot type], on [Shoot date], at [Venue]. Coverage: [Coverage].

## 2. What is included

- coverage by [Photographer count] photographers;
- at least [Edited images] edited, high-resolution images;
- an online gallery for viewing and download for [Gallery months] months; and
- [Extras].

## 3. Fees and deposit

The total fee is [Total fee]. A non-refundable booking deposit of [Deposit] is due on signing; the date is reserved only once it is paid. The balance of [Balance] is due [Balance due days] days before the shoot. Overtime is charged at [Overtime rate] per hour or part hour.

## 4. Client responsibilities

The Client will share a schedule and list of key people and shots two weeks before the shoot, arrange any venue permission for photography, and provide a meal and breaks during long coverage.

## 5. Delivery

Edited images will be delivered within [Delivery weeks] weeks through an online gallery. Selection and editing style are at the Photographer's professional discretion, consistent with the portfolio shown to the Client. Unedited files are not delivered.

## 6. Image rights

The Photographer keeps copyright in all images. The Client receives a personal, non-exclusive licence to print, share and post them, with credit where practical. Commercial use or resale needs the Photographer's written consent. The Photographer may use selected images in their portfolio; Client opts out: [Portfolio opt-out].

## 7. Cancellation and changes

If the Client cancels, the deposit is kept; if they cancel within [Late cancellation days] days of the shoot, [Late cancellation share] of the balance is also due. If the Photographer cannot attend, they will arrange a replacement of similar standard or refund all payments made.

## 8. Liability

The Photographer's liability is limited to the fees paid, and does not cover shots missed because of venue rules, weather, or guests' actions. This Contract is governed by [Governing law].
