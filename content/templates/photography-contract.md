# Photography Services Contract

This Contract is made on [Date] between **[Photographer or studio name]**, of [Photographer address] (the "Photographer"), and **[Client full name]**, of [Client address] (the "Client").

## 1. Booking details

Type of shoot: [Wedding, event, portrait, product or other]
Date: [Shoot date]
Location: [Venue name and address]
Coverage: [Start time] to [End time] ([Number] hours)

## 2. What is included

- [Number] hours of photography by [Number] photographer(s);
- a minimum of [Number] edited, high-resolution images;
- an online gallery for viewing and download for [Number] months; and
- [Any extras, e.g. a printed album or second shooter].

## 3. Fees and deposit

The total fee is [Currency and amount]. A non-refundable booking deposit of [Amount] is due on signing; the date is reserved only once it is paid. The balance of [Amount] is due [Number] days before the shoot. Overtime is charged at [Rate] per hour or part hour.

## 4. Client responsibilities

The Client will share a schedule and list of key people or shots by [Date], arrange any permits or venue permissions needed for photography, and provide a meal and breaks for shoots longer than [Number] hours.

## 5. Delivery

Edited images will be delivered within [Number] weeks of the shoot via an online gallery. Image selection and editing style are at the Photographer's professional discretion, consistent with the portfolio shown to the Client. Unedited files are not delivered.

## 6. Image rights

The Photographer keeps the copyright in all images. The Client receives a personal, non-exclusive licence to print, share and post the images, with credit where practical. Commercial use, resale or editing that changes the images requires the Photographer's written consent. The Photographer may use selected images in their portfolio and marketing unless the Client opts out here: [Opt out, yes or no].

## 7. Cancellation and changes

If the Client cancels, the deposit is kept, and if cancellation is within [Number] days of the shoot, [Percentage] of the balance is also due. Date changes are subject to availability. If the Photographer cannot attend because of illness or emergency, they will try to arrange a replacement of similar standard or refund all payments made.

## 8. Liability

The Photographer's liability is limited to the fees paid. The Photographer is not responsible for shots missed because of venue restrictions, weather, or guests' or the Client's actions. This Contract is governed by the laws of [Country or state].
