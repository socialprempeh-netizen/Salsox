about: |
  A promissory note is the simplest written record of a debt. The borrower signs a promise to pay a stated sum to the lender, on demand, on a date, or in instalments, with or without interest. It is common for loans between family members and friends, for a buyer paying a seller over time, and for small business loans where a full agreement would be more paper than the loan deserves.

  This template sets out the principal, the interest, the repayment terms and the final date by which everything must be paid. It allows early repayment, makes the whole balance payable if the borrower falls behind, and records that the borrower may not shift the debt to someone else without the lender's consent.

  The lender signs too, which is not strictly required for a note but gives both parties the same signed copy and confirms the terms were agreed. Check any interest cap where the borrower lives before setting a rate.

# Promissory Note

**Date:** [Note date]
**Amount:** [Principal amount]

## 1. Promise to pay

For value received, [Borrower name], of [Borrower address] (the "Borrower"), promises to pay [Lender name], of [Lender address] (the "Lender"), the sum of [Principal amount] (the "Principal"), with interest at [Interest rate] on the unpaid balance.

## 2. Repayment

The Borrower will repay the Principal and interest [Repayment terms]. Whatever remains unpaid, including interest, is due in full on [Final due date].

## 3. Prepayment

The Borrower may pay all or part of the balance early without penalty. Early payments reduce the Principal, and interest is charged only on what remains.

## 4. Late payment and acceleration

If any payment is more than [Days late] late, the Lender may give the Borrower written notice that the entire unpaid balance, with interest to the date of payment, is immediately due. The Lender's delay in using this right does not waive it.

## 5. Collection costs

If the Lender has to take steps to recover the balance after it becomes due, the Borrower will pay the Lender's reasonable costs of doing so, as far as the law allows.

## 6. Transfer

The Borrower may not transfer the obligation to pay to anyone else without the Lender's written consent.

## 7. Governing law

This note is governed by the law of [Governing law]. If any part of it is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
