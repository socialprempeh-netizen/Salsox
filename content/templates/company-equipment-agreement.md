about: |
  When a company hands an employee a laptop, phone or other equipment, both sides benefit from a short record of what was issued and the rules that come with it. It makes returning equipment at the end of employment straightforward, and it sets expectations for security, which matters because a lost laptop is often a data breach, not just a lost laptop.

  This template lists each item with its asset tag and serial number, says what personal use is allowed, and sets basic security rules: screen lock, updates, encryption, and reporting loss quickly. It treats accidental damage fairly, as the company's cost unless caused deliberately or through serious carelessness, and it explains how equipment is returned.

  It also tells the employee that company devices may be monitored for security and that they should not expect privacy on them. The law on workplace monitoring and on deductions from pay varies, so check yours, and keep the monitoring proportionate and described in your policies.

# Company Equipment Agreement

**Company:** [Company name]
**Employee:** [Employee name], [Job title]
**Date issued:** [Issue date]

## 1. Equipment issued

[Equipment list] (the "Equipment"). The Equipment belongs to the Company.

## 2. Use

The Equipment is provided for work. [Personal use rule]. The Employee will not lend the Equipment to anyone else, install unlicensed software on it, or use it for anything illegal.

## 3. Security

The Employee will [Security rules].

## 4. Loss, theft and damage

The Employee will report any loss, theft or damage to [IT contact] within [Loss report time], and report theft to the police. The Company bears the cost of normal wear and accidental damage. The Employee may be asked to contribute to the cost of repair or replacement only where loss or damage was deliberate or caused by serious carelessness.

## 5. Monitoring

The Company may monitor and manage the Equipment remotely for security and compliance, including locking or erasing it if it is lost. The Employee should not expect privacy in personal data stored on it, and should not store personal data there they want to keep private.

## 6. Return

The Employee will return all Equipment in working order, apart from normal wear, by [Return deadline], or sooner if asked. If Equipment is not returned within 14 days of that date, the Employee agrees that its depreciated value may be deducted from final pay, as far as the law allows.

## 7. Changes

Equipment added or replaced later will be recorded in an update to this list, signed by both parties.
