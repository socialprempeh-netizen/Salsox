# Sublease Agreement

This Sublease is made on [Agreement date] between **[Tenant name]** (the "Tenant") and **[Subtenant name]** (the "Subtenant"), with the consent of **[Landlord name]** (the "Landlord").

## 1. Head lease

The Tenant rents **[Property address]** from the Landlord under a lease dated [Head lease date] (the "Head Lease"). A copy has been given to the Subtenant.

## 2. What is sublet

The Tenant sublets to the Subtenant [Sublet part], from [Start date] to [End date]. This end date is not later than the end of the Head Lease.

## 3. Rent and deposit

The Subtenant will pay rent of [Rent], due on [Rent day], to [Payment to]. The Subtenant will pay the Tenant a deposit of [Deposit], returned at the end of the Sublease less any unpaid rent or cost of damage beyond normal wear and tear. The Subtenant pays for [Utilities].

## 4. Following the Head Lease

The Subtenant will follow every term of the Head Lease that applies to occupying the property, including its rules on noise, pets and alterations, and will not do anything that would put the Tenant in breach of it. The Subtenant may not sublet or assign further.

## 5. Responsibility

The Tenant remains responsible to the Landlord under the Head Lease. The Subtenant is responsible to the Tenant for any loss caused by the Subtenant or their guests.

## 6. Ending the Sublease

The Sublease ends on [End date], or earlier if the Head Lease ends. Either the Tenant or the Subtenant may end it early with [Notice period] written notice.
