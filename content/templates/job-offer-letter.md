faq:
  - q: "Is a signed offer letter the same as an employment contract?"
    a: "Not usually. An offer letter sets out the main terms and the candidate's acceptance; the employment contract that follows contains the full terms. In some places a signed offer can itself form a binding contract, so make sure the two agree."
  - q: "How long should I give a candidate to accept?"
    a: "Three to seven days is typical. Put the deadline in the letter so neither side is left waiting."
  - q: "Can the candidate sign the offer on their phone?"
    a: "Yes. Send the letter for e-signature and the candidate can review and sign it from any phone browser, which often speeds up acceptance."

# Offer of Employment

[Date]

[Candidate full name]
[Candidate address]

Dear [Candidate first name],

We are pleased to offer you the position of **[Job title]** at **[Company legal name]** (the "Company"). We were impressed by your experience and believe you will be a great addition to the [Team or department name] team.

## Position

You will report to [Manager name], [Manager title]. Your main responsibilities will include [Summarise key responsibilities]. A full job description is attached.

## Start date and location

Your employment will begin on [Start date]. You will be based at [Work location], with [Describe any remote or hybrid arrangement].

## Pay and benefits

Your starting salary will be [Currency and amount] per [Year or month], paid [Pay frequency] by bank transfer, less the deductions required by law. You will also be entitled to [List benefits, e.g. health insurance, pension contributions, bonus scheme].

## Hours and holidays

Your normal working hours are [Hours] per week, [Days and times]. You will receive [Number] days of paid holiday per year in addition to public holidays.

## Probation

The first [Number] months of your employment will be a probationary period, during which either party may end the employment with [Notice period] notice. After probation, the notice period will be [Notice period].

## Conditions of this offer

This offer is conditional on [List conditions, e.g. satisfactory references, proof of the right to work, background checks]. The full terms of your employment will be set out in your employment contract, which you will receive before your start date.

## Accepting this offer

To accept, please sign and return this letter by [Acceptance deadline]. If you have any questions, contact [Contact name] at [Contact email or phone].

We look forward to welcoming you.

Yours sincerely,

[Hiring manager name]
[Hiring manager title], [Company legal name]
Signature: ______________________ Date: __________

## Acceptance

I accept the offer of employment on the terms set out in this letter.
