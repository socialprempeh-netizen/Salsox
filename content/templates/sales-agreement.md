about: |
  A sales agreement records a sale of goods in writing, so that both sides agree on what is being sold, for how much, and on what terms before money or goods change hands. It matters most for larger orders, made-to-order goods and sales paid in instalments, where a misunderstanding about the specification, the delivery date or who carries the risk in transit is expensive to fix later.

  This template covers the terms a sales contract usually needs: a precise description and quantity, the price with any deposit and the balance terms, delivery address, date and cost, when ownership and risk pass to the buyer, a short inspection period, the warranty and what happens with faulty goods, and the law that applies. Replace each blank with your own details and remove anything that does not apply.

# Sales Agreement

**Date:** [Agreement date]

**Seller:** [Seller name], [Seller address]
**Buyer:** [Buyer name], [Buyer address]

## 1. Goods

The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to buy, the following goods: [Goods].

Quantity: [Quantity]   Unit price: [Unit price]

## 2. Price and payment

The total price is [Total price]. The Buyer pays a deposit of [Deposit], and [Balance terms]. Payment is made to the account or payment link the Seller provides. Late payments may carry interest at a reasonable rate after written notice.

## 3. Delivery

The Seller delivers the goods to [Delivery address] on or before [Delivery date]. Delivery costs are [Delivery costs]. If delivery will be late, the Seller tells the Buyer as soon as it knows and agrees a new date.

## 4. Ownership and risk

Risk in the goods passes to the Buyer on delivery. Ownership passes to the Buyer when the total price has been paid in full.

## 5. Inspection and acceptance

The Buyer inspects the goods within [Inspection period] of delivery and tells the Seller in writing of any shortage, damage or defect. Goods not reported within that period are treated as accepted, except for defects that could not reasonably have been found on inspection.

## 6. Warranty and faulty goods

The Seller warrants the goods for [Warranty] from delivery. During that time the Seller will repair or replace faulty goods, or refund the price paid for them, at the Seller's choice. The warranty does not cover misuse, accidental damage or normal wear.

## 7. Cancellation

The Buyer may cancel before the goods are made or dispatched; the Seller may keep from the deposit any costs it has reasonably incurred. After dispatch, the order may only be cancelled by agreement.

## 8. General

This agreement is the whole agreement between the parties for this sale. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both. It is governed by [Governing law].
