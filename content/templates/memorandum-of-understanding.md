about: |
  An MOU is how organisations agree to work together before they are ready for a full contract, or when a contract would be heavier than the relationship needs. Universities, NGOs, hospitals and companies use them to launch programmes, pilots and partnerships.

  The most important clause in an MOU is the one most often left out: which parts are binding. This template is explicit. The collaboration itself is a statement of intent, but confidentiality, use of each other's names and logos, and each party bearing its own costs are binding. That avoids an MOU being treated as a contract nobody meant to sign, or as worthless when a commitment does matter.

  The rest is practical: the purpose, what each party contributes, a timeline, named contacts and a regular review. When the collaboration firms up, a formal agreement can replace it. Both organisations sign through {site}.

# Memorandum of Understanding

This Memorandum of Understanding (the "MOU") is made on [MOU date] between **[Party A name]** and **[Party B name]** (each a "Party").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to work together on [Purpose].

## 2. Contributions

[Party A name] will contribute [Party A contribution].

[Party B name] will contribute [Party B contribution].

Together, the Parties will undertake [Shared commitment].

## 3. Timeline and review

This MOU runs from [Start date] to [End date]. The Parties will review progress [Review frequency] and may extend or change it in writing.

## 4. Contacts

[Party A name]: [Party A contact]. [Party B name]: [Party B contact].

## 5. Nature of this MOU

This MOU records the Parties' shared intentions. Except for clauses 6 to 8, it is not legally binding and does not create a partnership, joint venture or obligation to enter a further agreement. Either Party may withdraw by written notice.

## 6. Confidentiality (binding)

Each Party will keep confidential any non-public information received from the other in connection with this MOU.

## 7. Names and logos (binding)

Neither Party will use the other's name or logo without prior written approval, including in publicity about the collaboration.

## 8. Costs (binding)

Each Party bears its own costs unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.
