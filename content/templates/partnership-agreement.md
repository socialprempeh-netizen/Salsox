about: |
  Partnerships usually start on trust and end on paper. The time to agree how profits are split, who can sign for the business, and what happens if one partner leaves is at the start, when everyone is getting on well.

  This template records each partner's contribution, the profit split, who is responsible for what, and which decisions need everyone's agreement: borrowing, taking on a new partner, spending over a set limit, or selling the business. It sets monthly drawings against profits, keeps proper accounts, and gives each partner access to them.

  The exit clause is the most valuable part. It sets the notice a leaving partner gives, how their share is valued (by an independent accountant, not by argument), and how it is paid, so the business is not crippled by a departure. A mediation step comes before any court. Every partner signs and initials the exit clause.

# Partnership Agreement

This Partnership Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Partner A name]** and **[Partner B name]** (the "Partners").

## 1. The partnership

The Partners carry on business together as **[Partnership name]**, in [Business], from [Principal place].

## 2. Contributions

[Partner A name] contributes [Partner A contribution]. [Partner B name] contributes [Partner B contribution]. Contributions are recorded in each Partner's capital account and do not earn interest.

## 3. Profits and losses

Profits and losses are shared [Profit split]. Each Partner may draw [Monthly drawings] on account of profit.

## 4. Roles

[Partner A name] leads [Partner A role]. [Partner B name] leads [Partner B role]. Each Partner will work in the business in good faith and will not run a competing business.

## 5. Decisions

Day-to-day decisions are made by the Partner responsible. The agreement of all Partners is needed to borrow money, admit a new partner, spend more than [Spending limit] on a single item, or sell the business.

## 6. Accounts

The Partners keep proper accounts, open to each Partner at any time, prepared to [Accounting year end] each year.

## 7. Leaving the partnership

A Partner may leave by giving [Exit notice] written notice. The remaining Partners may buy the leaving Partner's share at a value set by [Valuation method], paid over [Payment period].
