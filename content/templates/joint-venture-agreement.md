about: |
  A joint venture lets two businesses combine what each does best for a specific project, without merging. This template creates a contractual joint venture: the parties work together under this agreement, without setting up a new company. That suits a defined project with a natural end, like a development, a product launch or a bid for a large contract.

  It sets out what each party contributes, how costs are paid and recovered, and how profit is split. Decisions go through a small steering group, with the most important ones requiring both parties to agree. Money moves through a dedicated account with dual signatories for larger payments, which is the single best protection against misunderstandings.

  It also covers what each brings and what is created: each keeps the intellectual property it brought, and work created for the venture is licensed to both for the project. A deadlock procedure and a clear list of ways the venture ends complete it. For a long-running venture with shared employees or outside investment, a joint venture company with a shareholders' agreement is usually the better structure.

# Joint Venture Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Party one name] and [Party two name] (the "Parties").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties will work together for [Venture purpose] (the "Project"). The joint venture is limited to the Project.

## 2. Contributions

- [Party one name] will contribute [Party one contribution].
- [Party two name] will contribute [Party two contribution].

## 3. Costs and profit

[Cost rule]. After costs are repaid, profit from the Project is shared [Profit split]. Losses are shared in the same proportions, unless caused by one Party's breach.

## 4. Management

A steering group of [Steering members] manages the Project and meets at least monthly. Day-to-day decisions are made by the Party responsible for that work. These decisions need both Parties' written agreement: [Reserved decisions].

## 5. Money

All Project money passes through [Bank account]. Each Party may inspect the account and Project records at any time, and a statement is shared monthly.

## 6. Intellectual property

Each Party keeps the intellectual property it brings to the Project. Work created for the Project is licensed to both Parties for the Project only, without charge.

## 7. Deadlock

If the steering group cannot agree on a reserved decision within 14 days, the issue goes to each Party's chief executive. If they cannot agree within a further 14 days, the Parties will try mediation before any other action.

## 8. Relationship

The Parties are independent. Nothing in this agreement makes them partners, and neither may make commitments for the other except as agreed in writing.

## 9. Timetable and ending

The Parties aim to complete the Project by [Target completion]. The joint venture ends when the Project is complete and profits are distributed, when both Parties agree, or if one Party becomes insolvent or materially breaches this agreement and does not fix it within 30 days.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other's confidential information private and use it only for the Project.

## 11. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of [Governing law].
