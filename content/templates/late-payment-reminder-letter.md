about: |
  Most late invoices are paid after a clear, polite reminder. The letter that works states the facts, makes paying easy, gives a firm date, and leaves room for a customer who is struggling to agree a plan rather than go silent.

  This template names the invoice, amount and original due date, gives the payment details, sets a final date, and explains the interest and recovery costs the law allows for late commercial payment. It offers a payment plan and invites the customer to sign and commit to a payment date, which turns a reminder into a written promise.

  Interest rules differ by country: in the UK, business-to-business debts carry statutory interest, and the EU has similar rules. Do not claim statutory interest on a debt owed by a consumer; use the terms in your contract instead.

# Payment Reminder

[Letter date]

To: [Contact name], [Customer name]
From: [Business name]

## Invoice overdue

Our records show that invoice [Invoice number], dated [Invoice date], for [Amount due] was due on [Due date] and has not yet been paid. If you have paid it in the last few days, thank you, and please ignore this letter.

## How to pay

Please pay the full amount by [Final date], by [Payment details].

## Interest and costs

Under the terms agreed and the law, we are entitled to charge [Interest terms] from the due date, together with a fixed sum for the cost of recovering the debt. We would much rather not, and will not add them if the invoice is paid by the final date above.

## If you cannot pay in full

If paying the full amount by that date is difficult, please contact us before then. We are happy to agree a payment plan in writing, in instalments that suit both of us.

## If the invoice is disputed

If you believe any part of the invoice is wrong, please tell us in writing which part and why, and pay the rest. We will look into it promptly.

## Next steps

If we do not hear from you by the final date, we may pass the matter to a collection agency or start legal proceedings to recover the debt, which would add to the costs. We hope that will not be necessary.

For the business: [[signature:business]]   Date: [[date:business]]
Name: [[name:business]]

## Customer's commitment

I acknowledge the amount above is owed and will pay it in full by the final date, or will contact you before then to agree a payment plan.

For the customer: [[signature:customer]]   Date: [[date:customer]]
Name: [[name:customer]]
