about: |
  An event planner sits between the client and every supplier, so the contract needs to make clear who is responsible for what. This template sets the planner's services, the budget they work within, and the rule that matters most: supplier contracts are made in the client's name and paid by the client, with the planner coordinating rather than buying on the client's behalf. That keeps the client's money and protections where they belong.

  It also covers what tends to go wrong: final guest numbers arriving late, the client making decisions directly with suppliers, a postponement, or a cancellation. The retainer is non-refundable because the planner turns away other bookings for the date; later payments are refundable on a sliding scale depending on how close to the event a cancellation comes.

  Use it for weddings, parties, conferences and corporate events. For a corporate client, add a purchase order number or an internal approver as a signer.

## 2. Services

The Planner will provide [Services]. Anything not listed is outside this contract and may be added by written agreement.

## 3. Budget

The Planner will work within [Client budget] and will tell the Client in writing before recommending anything that would exceed it.

## 4. Suppliers

Contracts with the venue and other suppliers will be made directly between the Client and each supplier, and the Client pays suppliers directly. The Planner coordinates suppliers but is not responsible for a supplier's failure to perform, although it will help find a replacement where it can. The Planner will disclose any commission it receives from a supplier.

## 5. Decisions

The Client will make decisions the Planner needs within 7 days of being asked. Changes the Client agrees directly with suppliers must be shared with the Planner promptly.

## 6. Final numbers

The Client will confirm final guest numbers and any dietary needs by [Final numbers date].

## 7. Fee

The Planner's fee is [Planning fee], payable as [Payment schedule].

## 8. Cancellation and postponement

If the Client cancels, the retainer is not refundable, payments made more than 90 days before the event are refunded at 50%, and payments made within 90 days are not refunded. If the Client postpones to a date within 12 months that the Planner can accept, payments made transfer to the new date. If the Planner cannot deliver the services for reasons within its control, it will refund all fees paid.

## 9. Circumstances outside anyone's control

Neither party is responsible for failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as severe weather, a venue closure ordered by authorities or a public health restriction. The parties will try in good faith to move the event first.

## 10. Liability

The Planner's total liability under this contract is limited to the fee paid, except for death or injury caused by negligence or anything that cannot be limited by law.
