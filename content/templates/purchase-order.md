about: |
  A purchase order is the buyer's written instruction to buy. It protects both sides: the buyer knows exactly what was ordered and at what price, and the supplier has written authority before committing stock or starting work. Most accounts teams will not pay an invoice without a matching PO.

  This template has the parts that make three-way matching work (PO number, line items with quantities and unit prices, delivery details, and payment terms) plus the approval that shows the order was authorised. The supplier signs to accept it, which turns the order into a contract on these terms, and quotes the PO number on their invoice.

  For regular supplies, sign a supplier agreement once and issue POs against it. With {site}, the supplier accepts the order from their phone and both sides keep the signed copy.
