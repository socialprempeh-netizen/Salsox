about: |
  Early-stage companies often add advisors: experienced people who give a few hours a month in exchange for a small amount of equity. The arrangement works best when it is specific. This template says what help the company expects and roughly how much time, which avoids the common outcome of an advisor holding equity for advice that never came.

  Equity vests monthly while the advisor continues, so it tracks the help actually given, and vesting stops if either side ends the relationship. The equity itself is granted through the company's equity plan and its board approval, which this agreement refers to rather than replaces. The advisor keeps company information confidential, assigns to the company any work they create for it, and discloses conflicts.

  Securities and tax rules apply to equity grants, and they differ by country. Have the grant approved and documented properly, and suggest the advisor takes their own tax advice.

# Advisor Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Company name] (the "Company") and [Advisor name] (the "Advisor").

## 1. Services

The Advisor will advise the Company on [Advisory focus]. The Advisor expects to give [Time commitment]. The Advisor decides how to provide the advice.

## 2. Equity

Subject to approval by the Company's board, the Company will grant the Advisor [Equity grant]. The grant vests [Vesting schedule], as long as the Advisor continues to provide services. The grant is governed by the equity plan and the grant documents, which prevail over this agreement if they conflict.

## 3. Expenses

The Company will repay [Expenses]. No other fees are payable.

## 4. Confidentiality

The Advisor will keep the Company's non-public information confidential and use it only to advise the Company, during this agreement and afterwards. This does not apply to information that is public or that the Advisor already had lawfully.

## 5. Work product

Anything the Advisor creates specifically for the Company under this agreement belongs to the Company. The Advisor keeps their general knowledge, skills and experience.

## 6. Conflicts

The Advisor will tell the Company of any role with a competitor or any other conflict of interest, now or in future. Advising other companies is otherwise not restricted.

## 7. Relationship

The Advisor is an independent contractor, not an employee, and has no authority to make commitments on the Company's behalf.

## 8. Term and ending

This agreement lasts for [Term]. Either party may end it earlier with 30 days' written notice. Vesting stops on the date it ends; sections 4 and 5 continue afterwards.

## 9. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of [Governing law].
