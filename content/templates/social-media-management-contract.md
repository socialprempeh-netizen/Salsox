about: |
  Social media management is a retainer, not a project: the same work repeats every month, so the contract needs to say what a month includes. This template sets the platforms, the number of posts, how content is approved before it goes out, and how quickly comments and messages are answered.

  It deals with the two things clients worry about most. First, the brand's voice: nothing is published without approval through a monthly calendar, and sensitive messages, such as complaints or questions that need a professional answer, are passed to the client instead of being answered by the manager. Second, money for ads: the ad budget is paid by the client directly to the platforms and capped per month, so the manager's fee and the ad spend never get mixed.

  Access is handled properly too: the manager is added as a user through each platform's business tools, never by sharing the client's password, and is removed when the contract ends.

# Social Media Management Contract

This contract is made on [Agreement date] between [Manager name] (the "Manager") and [Client name] (the "Client"). It starts on [Start date].

## 1. Services

The Manager will manage the Client's accounts on [Platforms], providing:

- content: [Monthly posts], written and designed by the Manager;
- community management: [Community management]; and
- a monthly report covering [Report contents].

## 2. Approval

Content follows [Approval process]. Nothing is published without the Client's approval. Urgent posts outside the calendar need the Client's approval in writing, which may be by message.

## 3. Sensitive messages

The Manager will follow this rule: [Escalation rule]. The Client is responsible for answering escalated messages.

## 4. Advertising

Ad spend is [Ad budget]. The Manager will not spend beyond the approved amount and is not responsible for the platforms' charges.

## 5. Access

The Client will give the Manager access through each platform's business or role-based tools. Neither party will share personal passwords. The Client keeps ownership of all accounts, pages and followers.

## 6. Fees

The Client will pay [Monthly fee] a month, invoiced in advance and due within 14 days. Extra work, such as photo shoots or video production, is quoted separately.

## 7. Content and ownership

The Client owns the content the Manager creates under this contract once it is paid for. The Manager may use published posts in its portfolio. Stock images and music are used under their own licences.

## 8. Term and ending

This contract has a minimum term of [Minimum term]. After that, either party may end it with [Notice period] written notice. On ending, the Manager will hand over the content calendar and remove its own access within 5 business days.

## 9. Responsibility

The Client is responsible for the accuracy of facts, offers and claims it approves. The Manager is not responsible for changes the platforms make to their rules, reach or features.

## 10. Governing law

This contract is governed by the law of [Governing law].
