# Commercial Lease Agreement

This Lease is made on [Lease date] between **[Landlord name]** (the "Landlord") and **[Tenant name]** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises and use

The Landlord lets to the Tenant [Premises] (the "Premises"), with the right to use the building's shared entrances and parking. The Tenant may use the Premises only as [Permitted use].

## 2. Term

The Lease starts on [Start date] and runs for [Term years] years.

## 3. Rent, service charge and review

Rent is [Annual rent] per year, paid [Rent instalments]. The Tenant also pays a service charge of [Service charge]. Rent will be reviewed [Rent review].

## 4. Deposit

The Tenant will pay a deposit of [Deposit] on signing, returned at the end of the Lease less any amounts owed.

## 5. Fit-out and signage

The Tenant may carry out a fit-out during [Fit-out period], to plans the Landlord approves, acting reasonably. The Tenant may display signage at the shopfront with the Landlord's approval, and will remove it and make good at the end of the Lease.

## 6. Repairs

The Landlord will keep the structure, roof and shared areas in repair. The Tenant will keep the inside of the Premises, including fixtures it installs, in good repair and decoration.

Tenant initials: [[initials:tenant]]

## 7. Insurance and compliance

The Landlord insures the building. The Tenant insures its contents and holds public liability cover, and complies with all licences and regulations for its business.

## 8. Break clause

The Tenant may end the Lease at [Break date] by giving [Break notice] written notice, provided rent is paid up to that date.

Tenant initials: [[initials:tenant]]

## 9. Assignment and governing law

The Tenant may not assign or sublet without the Landlord's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. This Lease is governed by [Governing law].
