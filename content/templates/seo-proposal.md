about: |
  SEO proposals lose clients in two ways: promising rankings nobody can guarantee, and describing activity instead of results. This template does neither. It opens with what an audit of the client's own site found, which shows you have looked and gives the plan its reasons, then sets goals tied to the client's business, here bookings, with the metrics that will show progress each month.

  The plan separates a first month of fixes from the ongoing monthly work, because technical problems and thin pages need solving before new content can do its job. It lists what the client must provide, since SEO depends on access and approvals as much as on the agency, and it is honest about timing: meaningful results usually take three to six months.

  The proposal includes the commercial terms, and signing it accepts them, so a separate contract is not needed for straightforward retainers.
