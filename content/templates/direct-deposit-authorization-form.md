  Paying wages by direct deposit needs two things from the employee: accurate bank details and written authorization. This form collects both in one signed document, which payroll keeps on file and which protects the employer if a payment goes to the wrong account because of details the employee gave.

  The employee enters the account holder name, bank, routing or sort code, and account number, and chooses whether to split pay between two accounts. The authorization lets the employer correct a deposit made in error, as banking rules usually allow, and explains how to change or cancel it.

  Collect bank details only through a secure channel, and store the signed form with restricted access: it contains information that could be misused. Do not ask employees to email account numbers.

## 3. Authorization

I authorize the employer, and the bank, to correct any deposit made in error, including by reversing an amount paid to me that I was not owed.

I confirm the account details are correct and that I am an account holder. I understand the employer is not responsible for a payment sent to an account using incorrect details I have given.
