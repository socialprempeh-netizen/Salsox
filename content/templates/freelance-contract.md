# Freelance Services Agreement

This Agreement is made on [Date] between **[Freelancer name or business name]**, of [Freelancer address] (the "Freelancer"), and **[Client legal name]**, of [Client address] (the "Client").

## 1. Services

The Freelancer will provide the following services (the "Services"): [Describe the work, e.g. design of a five-page website including homepage, about, services, contact and blog templates]. Deliverables: [List each deliverable and its format].

Anything not listed here is outside the scope and will be quoted separately before any work starts.

## 2. Timeline

Work will start on [Start date] and the final deliverables will be delivered by [Delivery date], provided the Client supplies the materials and feedback described below on time. Delays in Client feedback move the delivery date by the same amount.

## 3. Client responsibilities

The Client will supply [Content, logins, brand assets or other materials] by [Date], give consolidated feedback within [Number] working days of each delivery, and name one person to approve the work.

## 4. Revisions

The fee includes [Number] rounds of revisions. Further revisions, or changes after approval, are charged at [Currency and hourly or fixed rate].

## 5. Fees and payment

The total fee is [Currency and amount]. A non-refundable deposit of [Percentage or amount] is due on signing, and work begins once it is received. The balance is due [On delivery, or on a schedule], within [Number] days of invoice. Late payments may incur interest of [Rate] per month, or the maximum the law allows if lower.

## 6. Expenses

Pre-approved expenses, such as stock images, fonts or third-party software, will be invoiced at cost.

## 7. Ownership

When the Client has paid all fees in full, the Freelancer transfers ownership of the final deliverables to the Client. The Freelancer keeps ownership of their pre-existing tools, code and working files, and may show the work in their portfolio unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

## 8. Independent contractor

The Freelancer is an independent contractor, not an employee, and is responsible for their own taxes, equipment and working hours.

## 9. Confidentiality

Both parties will keep each other's confidential information private and use it only for this project.

## 10. Cancellation

Either party may end this Agreement with [Number] days' written notice. The Client will pay for all work done up to that date, and the deposit is not refunded.

## 11. Liability

The Freelancer's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid. This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Country or state].
