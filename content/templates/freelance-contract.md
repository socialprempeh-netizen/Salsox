# Freelance Services Agreement

This Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Freelancer name]**, of [Freelancer address] (the "Freelancer"), and **[Client legal name]**, of [Client address] (the "Client").

## 1. Services and deliverables

The Freelancer will provide [Services] (the "Services"). Deliverables: [Deliverables]. Anything not listed is outside the scope and will be quoted separately before any work starts.

## 2. Timeline

Work starts on [Start date] and final deliverables will be delivered by [Delivery date], provided the Client supplies [Client materials] by [Materials due date] and gives consolidated feedback within [Feedback days] working days of each delivery. Delays in Client feedback move the delivery date by the same amount.

## 3. Revisions

The fee includes [Revision rounds] rounds of revisions. Further revisions, or changes after approval, are charged at [Extra work rate].

## 4. Fees and payment

The total fee is [Total fee]. A non-refundable deposit of [Deposit] is due on signing, and work begins once it is received. The balance is due on delivery, within [Payment days] days of invoice. Late payments may incur interest of [Late interest], or the maximum the law allows if lower.

## 5. Ownership

When the Client has paid all fees, the Freelancer transfers ownership of the final deliverables to the Client. The Freelancer keeps their pre-existing tools and working files, and may show the work in their portfolio unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

## 6. Independent contractor and confidentiality

The Freelancer is an independent contractor, responsible for their own taxes, equipment and hours. Both parties will keep each other's confidential information private.

## 7. Cancellation and liability

Either party may end this Agreement with [Cancellation notice days] days' written notice; the Client pays for work done to that date and the deposit is not refunded. The Freelancer's total liability is limited to the fees paid. This Agreement is governed by [Governing law].
