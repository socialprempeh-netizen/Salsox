about: |
  When an employer pays for an expensive course, it is reasonable to ask for some of the money back if the employee leaves straight afterwards. A training repayment agreement, sometimes called a clawback, sets that out in advance, so nobody is surprised.

  The key to a fair, and enforceable, agreement is proportion. This template repays only the actual cost of the training, never a penalty, and the amount tapers to zero over a stated period, reflecting the value the employer has already received. It does not apply if the employer dismisses the employee for reasons other than misconduct, or if the employee leaves because of the employer's serious breach. Deductions from final pay are made only with the employee's written agreement, given here, and never below the minimum wage where that applies.

  Sign it before the training starts and before the employer pays, which matters legally in many countries and is fairer in every one.

# Training Repayment Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Employer name] (the "Employer") and [Employee name] (the "Employee"), employed as [Job title].

## 1. The training

The Employer will pay for the Employee to complete [Training name], provided by [Training provider] (the "Training"). The cost to the Employer is [Training cost] (the "Training Cost").

## 2. Support

The Employer will also give [Study leave].

## 3. Repayment if the Employee leaves

If the Employee resigns, or is dismissed for gross misconduct, within [Repayment period], the Employee will repay part of the Training Cost on this scale: [Repayment scale]. The amount is measured from the date the Training is completed, or from the date the Employee stops attending it if earlier.

## 4. When no repayment is due

No repayment is due if:

- the Employer dismisses the Employee for any reason other than gross misconduct, including redundancy;
- the Employee resigns because of a serious breach of contract by the Employer;
- the Employee leaves because of ill health, supported by medical evidence; or
- the Employer decides to withdraw the Training.

## 5. How repayment is made

The Employee agrees that any repayment due may be deducted from their final pay, to the extent the law allows and never so as to reduce pay below any statutory minimum. Any balance is payable within 60 days of leaving, or by instalments agreed in writing.

## 6. Only actual costs

The repayment will never exceed the Training Cost actually paid by the Employer, less any amount the Employer recovered from a grant or tax relief.
