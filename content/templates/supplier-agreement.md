  When you buy the same goods from the same supplier every month, a one-off purchase order for each delivery is not enough. A supplier agreement sets the standing terms once (pricing, delivery, quality, returns, payment) so each order is just quantities and dates.

  This template is written from the buyer's side but fair to both. The supplier commits to the specifications and to delivering within a set lead time, bears the risk until goods arrive, and replaces faulty goods. The buyer gives a forecast (not a commitment), inspects deliveries within a short window, and pays on fixed terms. Prices are fixed for a period, then reviewed with notice, which both sides can plan around.

  It runs for a term and can be ended with notice. Sign it through {site}, then send purchase orders against it.

# Supplier Agreement

This Supplier Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Buyer legal name]** (the "Buyer") and **[Supplier legal name]** (the "Supplier").

## 1. Products

The Supplier will supply [Products] (the "Products"). The Buyer's current forecast is [Forecast]. Forecasts are for planning only; the Buyer is committed only by purchase orders.

## 2. Orders

The Buyer places orders in writing, including by email, at least [Order lead time] before the delivery date. An order is accepted unless the Supplier rejects it within one working day.

## 3. Prices

Prices are set by [Price basis]. Prices include packaging and delivery.

## 4. Delivery

The Supplier delivers to [Delivery place], [Delivery terms]. Ownership passes to the Buyer on delivery. The Supplier tells the Buyer at once of any expected delay.

## 5. Quality and returns

The Products will match their specifications and be fit for purpose. The Buyer may inspect each delivery within [Inspection days] and reject any Products that do not conform. The Supplier will collect, replace or credit rejected Products at its own cost.

## 6. Payment

The Supplier invoices on delivery. The Buyer pays correct invoices within [Payment days] days.

## 7. Term and termination

This Agreement starts on [Start date] and runs for [Term], then continues until either party gives [Notice days] days' written notice. Either may end it at once for a serious breach not remedied within 30 days.

## 8. General

Each party keeps the other's confidential information private. This Agreement is governed by [Governing law] and may be signed electronically.
