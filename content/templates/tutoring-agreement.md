# Tutoring Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Tutor name] (the "Tutor") and [Parent name] (the "Parent"), for lessons for [Student name], in [Student year] (the "Student").

## 1. Subjects and goals

The Tutor will teach [Subjects]. The shared aim is to [Goals]. Results depend on many things, including the Student's work between lessons, and are not guaranteed.

## 2. Schedule and location

Lessons are [Schedule], held [Location].

## 3. Fees

The fee is [Rate]. Single lessons are paid by bank transfer within 7 days of the lesson. Packages are used within six months of purchase.

## 4. Cancellations by the family

Lessons cancelled with at least [Cancellation notice] notice can be rescheduled at no charge. Later cancellations, or missed lessons, are charged in full.

## 5. Cancellations by the tutor

If the Tutor cancels, the lesson will be rescheduled at no charge, or refunded if no new time suits.

## 6. Safeguarding

For online lessons, the Student will join from a shared or open space at home, and lessons will not be recorded without the Parent's written consent. For lessons at home, a parent or another responsible adult will be in the home. The Tutor holds a current background check, available on request, and will follow safeguarding good practice at all times.

## 7. Progress

The Tutor will share [Progress updates]. The Parent may raise any concern with the Tutor at any time.

## 8. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement with one week's notice. Unused lessons in a prepaid package are refunded.
