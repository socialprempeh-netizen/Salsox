# Lease Renewal Agreement

This Lease Renewal Agreement (the "Renewal") is made on [Date] between **[Landlord full name or company]** (the "Landlord") and **[Tenant full name(s)]** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Original lease

The Landlord and the Tenant are parties to a lease dated [Original lease date] for the property at **[Property address]** (the "Lease"), which is due to end on [Current end date].

## 2. Renewal term

The parties agree to renew the Lease for a further term beginning on [New start date] and ending on [New end date] (the "Renewal Term").

## 3. Rent

During the Renewal Term, the rent will be [Currency and amount] per month, [Unchanged from, or increased from] the current rent of [Current rent], payable on the same day and in the same way as under the Lease.

## 4. Deposit

The deposit of [Currency and amount] currently held under the Lease will continue to be held on the same terms. [If the deposit changes, state the new amount and when any top-up is due.]

## 5. Other changes

The following terms of the Lease are changed for the Renewal Term:

- [Describe change 1, or write "None"]; and
- [Describe change 2, if any].

## 6. Everything else unchanged

Except as set out in this Renewal, every term of the Lease remains in full force and applies during the Renewal Term. If this Renewal and the Lease conflict, this Renewal applies.

## 7. Condition of the property

The Tenant confirms that the property is in the condition recorded in [The original inventory, or an updated condition report dated Date], subject to normal wear and tear.

## 8. Notice at the end of the Renewal Term

To end the tenancy at the end of the Renewal Term, either party must give [Notice period] written notice, or as otherwise required by law.

## 9. Governing law

This Renewal is governed by the same law as the Lease and may be signed electronically.
