about: |
  Most new employees see information on their first day that the business could not afford to have walk out of the door: patient or customer records, prices, supplier terms, code. An employee NDA puts the rules in writing before access is granted.

  This one is written for a small employer. It defines company information in plain terms, separates duties during employment from the duties that survive it, asks for company property and files back when the job ends, and confirms that work created on the job belongs to the business. It also says, explicitly, that nothing in it stops the employee reporting wrongdoing to a regulator, which many countries require and which keeps the rest of the agreement enforceable.

  Send it with the offer letter or employment contract so both are signed before the start date.

# Employee Confidentiality Agreement

This Confidentiality Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Agreement date] between **[Company legal name]**, of [Company address] (the "Company"), and **[Employee full name]**, of [Employee address] (the "Employee").

## 1. Background

The Employee is employed by the Company as [Job title] starting on [Start date]. In that role the Employee will have access to information the Company needs to keep confidential. This Agreement is in addition to the Employee's employment contract.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means information about the Company, its customers, patients, suppliers and staff that is not public, including customer and pricing data, records, business plans, software, product designs, processes, personnel records and anything marked confidential. It does not include information that becomes public other than through the Employee's breach.

## 3. Duties during employment

The Employee will use Confidential Information only to do their job, disclose it outside the Company only as their role requires, follow the Company's security policies, and not copy or remove Confidential Information from Company systems except as their work needs.

## 4. Duties after employment

The duty not to use or disclose Confidential Information continues after employment ends, for [Survival in years] years, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain secret. This does not prevent the Employee from using general skills and experience gained in the role.

## 5. Company property

On leaving, or whenever the Company asks, the Employee will return all Company property, including laptops, phones, access cards, documents and files, and permanently delete any Company information held on personal devices or accounts.

## 6. Work created

Work the Employee creates in the course of employment belongs to the Company to the extent allowed by law.

## 7. Protected disclosures

Nothing in this Agreement stops the Employee from reporting a suspected crime or wrongdoing to a regulator or the authorities, or from making any disclosure protected by law.

## 8. General

This Agreement is governed by [Governing law] and may be signed electronically. If any part is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
