# Employee Confidentiality Agreement

This Confidentiality Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Date] between **[Company legal name]**, of [Company address] (the "Company"), and **[Employee full name]**, of [Employee address] (the "Employee").

## 1. Background

The Employee is employed by the Company as [Job title] starting on [Start date]. In that role the Employee will have access to information the Company needs to keep confidential. This Agreement is in addition to the Employee's employment contract.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means information about the Company, its customers, suppliers and staff that is not public, including customer and pricing data, sales figures, business plans, software and source code, product designs, processes, personnel records and any information marked confidential. It does not include information that becomes public other than through the Employee's breach.

## 3. Duties during employment

The Employee will use Confidential Information only to do their job; not disclose it to anyone outside the Company except as their role requires; follow the Company's security policies; and not copy or remove Confidential Information from Company systems except as needed for their work.

## 4. Duties after employment

The Employee's duty not to use or disclose Confidential Information continues after their employment ends, for [Number] years, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain secret. This does not prevent the Employee from using general skills and experience gained in the role.

## 5. Company property

On leaving, or whenever the Company asks, the Employee will return all Company property, including laptops, phones, access cards, documents and files, and permanently delete any Company information held on personal devices or accounts, confirming this in writing if asked.

## 6. Work created

Work the Employee creates in the course of their employment belongs to the Company to the extent allowed by law, and the Employee will sign any documents reasonably needed to confirm this.

## 7. Protected disclosures

Nothing in this Agreement stops the Employee from reporting a suspected crime or wrongdoing to a regulator or the authorities, from making a disclosure protected by law, or from discussing their own pay and conditions where the law allows it.

## 8. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Country or state] and may be signed electronically. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
