about: |
  A managed IT services proposal sells an ongoing service, so it has to show the prospect that you understand how they work today and what will be different. Leading with their situation and goals, then the services that address them, makes the price easier to judge.

  This template moves from the client's current situation and goals to the services in scope, the response times you commit to, the onboarding plan, and the monthly price per user. It lists what is not included, so project work and hardware are not mistaken for part of the monthly fee, and ends with clear next steps and a signature to accept.

  Back the response times with a service level agreement; the proposal summarizes them and the agreement makes them binding.
