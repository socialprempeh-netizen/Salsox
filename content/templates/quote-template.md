faq:
  - q: "What is the difference between a quote and an estimate?"
    a: "A quote is usually a fixed price for the work described; an estimate is an approximate figure that may change. Label the document clearly and, for an estimate, say how much the final price may vary."
  - q: "How long should a quote be valid?"
    a: "Fourteen to thirty days is common. Material and labour costs change, so a validity date protects you from honouring an old price."
  - q: "Why get a quote signed?"
    a: "A signed quote is the client's written approval of scope and price. It turns the quote into an order and is what you refer to if the scope changes later."

**From:** [Your business name]
[Your address]
[Your email] | [Your phone]

**Prepared for:** [Client legal name]
[Client address]
[Client contact name]

**Quote number:** [Quote number]
**Date:** [Quote date]
**Valid until:** [Expiry date]

## Summary

[One or two sentences describing the job, e.g. supply and installation of a 5 kW solar system at the Client's premises.]

## Itemised price

| Item | Quantity | Unit price | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Item, material or service 1] | [Qty] | [Price] | [Amount] |

**Subtotal:** [Currency and subtotal]
**Tax ([Tax rate]%):** [Tax amount]
**Total:** [Currency and total]

## Included

- [What the price includes, e.g. delivery, installation, one follow-up visit].

## Not included

- [What the price excludes, e.g. permits, structural work, anything not listed above].

## Timeline

Work can begin within [Number] days of acceptance and will take approximately [Duration].

## Terms

1. This quote is valid until [Expiry date]. After that, prices may change.
2. A deposit of [Percentage]% is due on acceptance, with the balance due [On completion or on a schedule].
3. Changes to the scope after acceptance will be quoted separately and agreed in writing before the work is done.
4. [Any other terms, e.g. warranty period or cancellation terms.]

## Acceptance
