about: |
  Background checks are routine for many roles, but they involve sensitive information, and most laws require the candidate's informed consent first. The candidate should know which checks will be run, by whom, why they are needed for this role, how long the results will be kept, and how they can see and correct what is found.

  This template sets that out in plain words and records the candidate's authorisation. It keeps the checks proportionate by tying them to the role, states that results are used only for the hiring decision, and explains the candidate's right to see the results and to respond before any decision is made on them.

  Rules differ widely. In the United States, consumer report checks need a stand-alone disclosure under federal law and some states add their own forms; in the UK, criminal record checks depend on the role's eligibility; across Europe, the GDPR limits criminal data processing. Adapt the wording, and the list of checks, to the law where the candidate is hired.

# Background Check Authorisation

**Employer:** [Employer name]
**Candidate:** [Candidate name]
**Role:** [Role applied for]

## 1. What will be checked

With your permission, the following checks will be carried out: [Checks included].

## 2. Who carries them out

The checks will be carried out by [Screening provider] on the employer's behalf. They will contact only the sources needed for the checks above.

## 3. Why

These checks are needed because [Reason for checks]. The results will be used only to decide on your application for this role.

## 4. Your information

The employer will keep the results securely for [Retention period], then delete them. They will be seen only by people involved in the hiring decision.

## 5. Your rights

- You may ask for a copy of the results by writing to [Privacy contact].
- If anything found might affect the decision, you will be told and given the chance to explain or correct it before the decision is made.
- You may withdraw this authorisation before the checks are completed, by writing to the same address.
