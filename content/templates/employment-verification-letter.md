about: |
  Landlords, mortgage lenders, banks and immigration offices often ask an employer to confirm that someone works there. An employment verification letter gives the facts they need on company letterhead, signed by someone with authority to confirm them.

  This template confirms the job title, start date, employment status and hours, and includes salary only with the employee's written authorization. Having the employee sign the same letter is the simplest way to record that permission, which data protection rules in many countries expect before personal information is shared with a third party.

  Keep the letter factual. Confirm only what the records show, avoid predictions about future employment, and do not add opinions about performance unless the employee asks for a reference instead.

# Employment Verification

[Letter date]

To: [Recipient]

## Confirmation of employment

I am writing on behalf of [Company name] to confirm the employment of [Employee name].

As of the date of this letter, the company records show the following:

| Detail | Record |
|---|---|
| Job title | [Job title] |
| Employed since | [Start date] |
| Status | [Employment status] |
| Salary | [Salary] |

## Scope of this letter

This letter confirms the facts above as of its date, from the company's employment records. It is not a reference, does not describe performance, and is not a promise about future employment or pay. It is provided at the employee's request, for the recipient named above only.

## Checking this letter

To confirm that this letter is genuine, please contact the HR team at [Contact details], quoting the employee's name. We will confirm the details above but cannot share other information without the employee's further consent.

Yours faithfully,
