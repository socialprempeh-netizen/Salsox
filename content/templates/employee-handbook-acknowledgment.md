## Acknowledgment

I confirm that:

- I have received a copy of the [Handbook version], either on paper or electronically;
- I have had the opportunity to read it and to ask questions about anything I did not understand;
- I understand that the policies in the handbook apply to me, including the policies on [Key policies];
- I understand that the handbook describes policies and procedures and is not a contract of employment, and that the company may update it, with notice of significant changes; and
- I know that questions about the handbook can be raised with HR at [HR contact].

I understand that breaking the company's policies may lead to disciplinary action, as described in the handbook. Where the handbook and my employment contract differ, my contract applies, and I will ask HR whenever a policy is unclear to me.
