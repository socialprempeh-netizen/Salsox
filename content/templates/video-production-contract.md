about: |
  Video projects have more moving parts than most creative work: a treatment to agree, locations and people to book, a shoot that depends on weather, an edit with rounds of feedback, and rights in music and in the faces on screen. A video production contract puts each in the right order with the right person responsible.

  This template lists the deliverables with their lengths and formats, makes pre-production approval the gate before anyone travels to a shoot, and sets the number of edit rounds included. It makes the producer responsible for clearing licensed music and getting release forms from everyone on camera, which protects the client's use of the film. It explains what happens if weather stops the shoot.

  Usage rights are stated precisely, because a film licensed for social media only cannot later run as a TV advert without new music and talent clearances. Raw footage stays with the producer unless the client pays for it.

# Video Production Contract

This contract is made on [Agreement date] between [Producer name] (the "Producer") and [Client name] (the "Client") for [Project] (the "Project").

## 1. Deliverables

The Producer will deliver [Deliverables] by [Delivery date], provided the Client gives approvals and feedback on time.

## 2. Pre-production

Before the shoot, the Producer will prepare [Pre-production]. The shoot goes ahead only once the Client has approved these.

## 3. Production

The Producer will film over [Shoot days]. The Producer is responsible for crew, equipment, insurance and location permissions.

## 4. Weather and postponement

[Weather rule]. A postponement requested by the Client for other reasons with less than 7 days' notice is charged at the crew's day rates already committed.

## 5. Edit and revisions

The fee includes [Revision rounds]. Each round is one consolidated set of feedback. Further rounds, or changes requiring new filming, are quoted separately.

## 6. Music and people

The Producer will license all music for the uses in section 8, and obtain signed releases from everyone recognisable on camera. The Client is responsible for the accuracy of product claims in the script it approves.

## 7. Fee

The fee is [Fee], payable [Payment schedule]. Invoices are due within 14 days.

## 8. Usage and ownership

Once the fee is paid in full, the Client may use the deliverables for [Usage rights]. The Producer keeps ownership of raw footage and project files, and may show the finished film in its portfolio after the Client's launch.

## 9. Cancellation

If the Client cancels after pre-production starts, the first payment is kept; if after the shoot, the second payment is also due, and the Producer will deliver the footage edited so far.

## 10. Governing law

This contract is governed by the law of [Governing law].
