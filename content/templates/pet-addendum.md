# Pet Addendum

This addendum is made on [Addendum date] between [Landlord name] (the "Landlord") and [Tenant name] (the "Tenant"), and forms part of the lease dated [Lease date] for [Property address] (the "Property").

## 1. Pet allowed

The Landlord consents to the Tenant keeping [Pet description] (the "Pet") at the Property. No other animal may be kept without the Landlord's further written consent.

## 2. Deposit and rent

Pet deposit: [Pet deposit]. Additional monthly pet rent: [Pet rent]. Any deposit is returned under the same rules as the main deposit, less any deduction for damage caused by the Pet.

## 3. Health and registration

The Tenant will keep the Pet vaccinated, treated for fleas and worms, and registered or microchipped as local law requires, and will show proof on request.

## 4. Behaviour and noise

The Tenant will keep the Pet under control in shared areas, on a lead where there is one, and will not let it cause a nuisance to neighbours, including persistent noise.

## 5. Waste and cleaning

The Tenant will clear up after the Pet straight away, inside and outside the Property. At the end of the tenancy, the Tenant will have carpets and soft furnishings professionally cleaned and treated for fleas.

## 6. Damage

The Tenant is responsible for any damage the Pet causes to the Property beyond fair wear and tear, and for any injury or damage it causes to others.

## 7. Withdrawing consent

If the Tenant breaches this addendum and does not put it right within 14 days of written notice, or the Pet is the subject of repeated complaints, the Landlord may withdraw consent with 30 days' written notice, and the Tenant will rehome the Pet.

## 8. The lease

All other terms of the lease remain in force. Where this addendum and the lease differ about the Pet, this addendum applies.
