# Photo and Video Release Form

**Organisation:** [Organisation name]
**Participant:** [Participant name]

## 1. What this covers

Photographs, video and audio recordings of the participant taken during [Shoot description] (the "Material").

## 2. Permission

The participant gives [Organisation name] permission to use, edit and publish the Material in [Permitted uses], for [Duration]. The Material will not be used in a way that is misleading or that the participant would reasonably find offensive, and will not be sold to third parties.

## 3. Compensation

In return the participant receives [Compensation]. No other payment is due for the use described above.

## 4. Withdrawing consent

The participant may withdraw consent for future use at any time by writing to [Withdrawal contact]. The organisation will stop new uses and remove the Material from its website and social media within 30 days. Printed materials already distributed cannot be recalled.

## 5. Personal information

The organisation will keep the participant's details only to manage this consent, and will not share them except as needed for the uses above.
