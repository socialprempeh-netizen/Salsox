about: |
  Ending a lease is simplest when the notice is in writing, names the right lease, and gives the date the tenancy ends. A clear letter avoids the usual arguments about when notice was given and when rent stops.

  This template is written from the tenant to the landlord, but works the other way round with the roles swapped. It cites the notice clause, gives the move-out date, asks for a final inspection, and gives a forwarding address and account for the deposit. Asking the landlord to sign and date it gives the tenant proof the notice was received, which is often more useful than proof of posting.

  Check the notice period in the lease and in local law before sending: a fixed-term lease may not allow early termination at all without a break clause or the landlord's agreement.

# Notice to End Tenancy

[Letter date]

Dear [Landlord name],

I am writing to give notice that I will end my tenancy of [Property address] (the "Property"), under the lease dated [Lease date].

## Notice and end date

This notice is given under [Notice clause]. My tenancy will end, and I will have moved out, on [Move-out date]. I will pay rent up to and including that date.

## Final inspection and keys

I would like to arrange a final inspection [Inspection time], so that we can walk through the Property together and agree its condition against the check-in inventory. I will return every key and access card at the inspection.

## Deposit

Please return my deposit, less any deductions agreed at the inspection, to [Deposit account]. If you propose any deduction, please send the reason and the evidence in writing so that I can respond.

## Contact after I move

Please send post and any correspondence about the tenancy to [Forwarding address]. I will also give you my updated contact details at the inspection.

## Meter readings and utilities

I will take final meter readings on the move-out date and send you a copy, and I will close the utility and council tax accounts in my name.

Thank you for your help during the tenancy. Please sign below to confirm you have received this notice.
