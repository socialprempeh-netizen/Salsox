# Employment Contract

This Employment Contract (the "Contract") is made on [Contract date] between **[Employer legal name]**, of [Employer address] (the "Employer"), and **[Employee full name]**, of [Employee address] (the "Employee").

## 1. Start date and role

The Employee's employment begins on [Start date]. The Employee is employed as **[Job title]** and will carry out the duties in the attached job description and any other reasonable duties that suit their skills.

## 2. Place of work

The Employee's normal place of work is [Work location].

## 3. Hours of work

Normal working hours are [Weekly hours] hours per week: [Working days and times]. The Employee may be asked to work reasonable additional hours when needed, paid as required by law.

## 4. Pay

The Employee will be paid [Salary], in arrears on [Pay day] by bank transfer, less tax and other deductions required by law. Pay will be reviewed once a year, with no obligation to increase it.

## 5. Holidays and sickness

The Employee is entitled to [Holiday days] working days of paid holiday per year, plus public holidays, agreed in advance with their manager. The Employee must report any absence on its first day. Sick pay is [Sick pay].

## 6. Probation and notice

The first [Probation months] months are a probationary period, during which either party may end the employment with [Probation notice] written notice. After probation, either party must give [Notice period] written notice, or more if the law requires. The Employer may end employment without notice for gross misconduct.

Employee initials: [[initials:employee]]

## 7. Confidentiality and property

The Employee will keep the Employer's confidential information secret during and after employment and return all company property when employment ends. Work created in the course of employment belongs to the Employer to the extent allowed by law.

## 8. Policies and law

The Employee will follow the Employer's policies, which do not form part of this Contract and may change. This Contract is governed by [Governing law].
