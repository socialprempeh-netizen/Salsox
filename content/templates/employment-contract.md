# Employment Contract

This Employment Contract (the "Contract") is made on [Date] between **[Company legal name]**, registered at [Company address] (the "Employer"), and **[Employee full name]**, of [Employee address] (the "Employee").

## 1. Start date and continuous employment

The Employee's employment begins on [Start date]. [Previous employment counting towards continuous service, if any, or "No previous employment counts towards continuous service."]

## 2. Job title and duties

The Employee is employed as **[Job title]** and will carry out the duties in the attached job description and any other reasonable duties the Employer assigns that suit the Employee's skills.

## 3. Place of work

The Employee's normal place of work is [Work location]. [Describe any remote, hybrid or travel requirements.]

## 4. Hours of work

Normal working hours are [Number] hours per week, [Days and times], with a [Length] unpaid break each day. The Employee may be asked to work reasonable additional hours when needed, [Paid or unpaid, and at what rate].

## 5. Pay

The Employee will be paid [Currency and amount] per [Year or month], in arrears on [Pay day] by bank transfer, less tax and other deductions required by law. Pay will be reviewed [Frequency], with no obligation to increase it.

## 6. Holidays

The Employee is entitled to [Number] working days of paid holiday per holiday year, which runs from [Start date of holiday year], plus public holidays. Holiday must be agreed in advance with the Employee's manager.

## 7. Sickness

The Employee must tell their manager as early as possible on the first day of any absence. Sick pay is [Describe sick pay, or "paid as required by law"].

## 8. Probation

The first [Number] months are a probationary period. During it, either party may end the employment by giving [Notice period] written notice.

## 9. Notice

After probation, the Employee must give [Notice period] written notice to resign and the Employer must give [Notice period], or more if the law requires. The Employer may end employment without notice for gross misconduct.

## 10. Confidentiality and property

The Employee will keep the Employer's confidential information secret during and after employment and return all company property when employment ends. Work created in the course of employment belongs to the Employer to the extent allowed by law.

## 11. Policies and law

The Employee will follow the Employer's policies, which do not form part of this Contract and may change. This Contract is governed by the laws of [Country or state].
