about: |
  Clients hire consultants to answer a question they cannot answer alone. A consulting proposal that states that question precisely, and shows a credible route to the answer, does most of the selling by itself.

  This template begins with the key question and three concrete objectives, then describes the approach in enough detail to be believable: what you will measure, who you will talk to, what you will test. It names the team, gives a timeline with checkpoints, and states the fee as a fixed price with capped expenses, which most clients prefer to open-ended day rates.

  The assumptions section protects you: it lists what the client must provide for the price and timeline to hold. When an assumption fails, you have a fair basis for a change. The client accepts by signing, through {site}, on any device.
