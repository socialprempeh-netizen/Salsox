about: |
  Commission disputes are almost always about definitions: when is a deal a deal, which number is the rate applied to, and what happens if the customer cancels or the rep leaves. A commission agreement written before the period starts answers those questions while nobody's money depends on the answer.

  This template defines the territory, the quota and what counts towards it, the base rate and an accelerator above quota, and the moment commission is earned, here when the customer has signed and paid, not when a contract is merely sent. It sets the payment schedule, a short clawback for customers who cancel and are refunded, how split deals and disputes are handled, and what happens to commission on deals in progress when the rep leaves.

  Many places have specific rules on commission for employees, including when unpaid commission must be paid on leaving and whether a written plan is required. Check the law where the rep works, and review the plan each year.

# Sales Commission Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Company name] (the "Company") and [Rep name] (the "Representative"). It applies to sales made during [Plan period] (the "Plan Period").

## 1. Territory

The Representative is responsible for [Territory].

## 2. Quota

The Representative's quota for the Plan Period is [Quota]. Only deals in the territory, approved by the Company at its standard terms or discounts it has authorised, count towards it.

## 3. Commission rates

- [Base rate] on deals up to quota.
- [Accelerator].

## 4. When commission is earned

Commission is earned [Earned point]. Commission is calculated on contract value excluding taxes, shipping, and any one-off fees for services the Company does not count as recurring revenue.

## 5. Payment

Earned commission is paid [Payment schedule], with a statement showing each deal and the calculation.

## 6. Clawbacks

If a customer cancels within [Clawback window] of commission being paid and the Company refunds the customer, the commission on the refunded amount is deducted from future commission. Deductions from wages are made only where the law allows.

## 7. Split deals and disputes

Where two representatives contribute to a deal, the sales manager decides the split before the deal closes. The Representative may raise any question about a statement within 30 days; the Company will review it and respond in writing within 15 business days.

## 8. Leaving the Company

If the Representative's employment ends, [Post-departure rule]. No commission is earned on deals signed after the last day of employment.

## 9. Changes

The Company may change this plan for future periods with 30 days' written notice. Changes do not affect commission already earned.

## 10. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of [Governing law].
