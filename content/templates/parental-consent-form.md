about: |
  Every trip outside school needs a parent's informed consent, and the form that records it should give parents what they need to decide: where, when, how students travel, who supervises, what it costs. This template puts those details first.

  It then collects what staff need on the day: medical conditions and medication, allergies and dietary needs, an emergency contact, and consent for emergency medical treatment if a parent cannot be reached. Photo consent is a separate yes or no, because many parents are happy for their child to go on the trip but not to appear on the school's social media.
