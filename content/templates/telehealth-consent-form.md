# Telehealth Consent

Practice: [Practice name]
Patient: [Patient name]   Date of birth: [Date of birth]

## 1. What telehealth is

Telehealth means receiving care by video or phone instead of in person. Appointments take place using [Platform].

## 2. Limits of remote care

The clinician cannot physically examine me during a telehealth visit. They may decide that an in-person visit, a test or a referral is needed, and may end a telehealth visit if remote care is not appropriate.

## 3. Privacy

The practice uses a platform with encryption and protects my information as it does for in-person care. Sessions are not recorded without my separate written consent. I will join from a private place, and I understand there are some risks with any electronic communication, despite these protections.

## 4. Location and emergencies

At the start of each visit, I will confirm where I am, so that help can be sent if I need it. Telehealth is not for emergencies: in an emergency, I will call [Emergency number].

## 5. If the connection fails

If the video connection fails, the clinician will call me on [Backup phone]. If we cannot reconnect, the appointment will be rescheduled.

## 6. My choices

Telehealth is voluntary. I may choose an in-person appointment instead, and I may withdraw this consent at any time, in writing, without it affecting my care.

## 7. Fees

Telehealth visits are billed in the same way as in-person visits, unless the practice tells me otherwise in advance. I am responsible for checking my insurance coverage.

## Consent

I have read this form, had the chance to ask questions, and consent to receive care by telehealth.
