# Consignment Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Shop name] (the "Shop") and [Consignor name] (the "Consignor").

## 1. Items

The Consignor places [Items] (the "Items") with the Shop for sale. Both parties have initialled the inventory sheet, which records each Item's condition and agreed price.

## 2. Ownership

The Consignor owns each Item until it is sold. The Items are not the Shop's property and are not available to the Shop's creditors.

## 3. Sale and pricing

The Shop will display the Items and sell them at the agreed prices. It will not sell below those prices without the Consignor's consent, except that [Price reduction].

## 4. Commission

The Shop keeps [Commission] on each Item sold. Card fees are paid by the Shop.

## 5. Payment

The Shop pays the Consignor's share [Payout schedule], with a statement listing each Item sold, its sale price and the commission kept.

## 6. Care of the Items

The Shop will take reasonable care of the Items. If an Item is lost, stolen or damaged while in the Shop's care, the Shop pays the Consignor the share they would have received at the agreed price.

## 7. Period and unsold Items

The Items stay in the Shop for [Consignment period]. The Consignor will collect unsold Items within 14 days after that. Items not collected 30 days after a written reminder may be donated to charity.

## 8. Ending early

Either party may end this agreement with 7 days' written notice. Sales made before the end are paid as normal, and the Consignor collects the remaining Items.
