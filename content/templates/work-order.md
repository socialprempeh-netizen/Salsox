about: |
  A work order is the paper trail of a job: it authorises the work before it starts and records what was done when it finishes. Maintenance teams, contractors and repair businesses use it to dispatch technicians, track parts, invoice accurately and settle any later question about what happened.

  This template covers both ends. The top half is the request: customer, site, contact, what is wrong, priority and when the technician will attend. The bottom half is the close-out: labour, parts and call-out fee priced on the spot, a description of the work done, and the customer's signature confirming the job is complete and the charges are agreed.

  That signature is what makes invoicing quick and disputes rare. With {site}, the customer signs on the technician's phone before they leave site, and the office has the signed work order at once.
