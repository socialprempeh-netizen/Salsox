# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Agreement date] between **[Party A legal name]**, of [Party A address] ("Party A"), and **[Party B legal name]**, of [Party B address] ("Party B"). Each is a "Party". The Party sharing information is the "Discloser" and the Party receiving it is the "Recipient".

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to share confidential information with each other for the purpose of [Purpose] (the "Purpose"). This Agreement sets out how that information will be protected.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including business plans, pricing, customer lists, financial information, technical data, product plans and know-how.

It does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was already lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's information.

## 3. Obligations

Each Recipient will use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose; disclose it only to its employees, officers and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by duties at least as strict as these; and protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care.

## 4. Required disclosure

A Recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or a court, provided it gives the Discloser prompt written notice where legally allowed and discloses only what is required.

## 5. Return or destruction

On written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so, except copies it must keep by law.

## 6. Term

This Agreement lasts [Term in years] years from the date above. Obligations for information disclosed during the term continue for [Survival in years] years after it ends, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 7. General

No licence in any Confidential Information is granted, and neither Party is obliged to enter into any further agreement. This Agreement is governed by [Governing law], is the entire agreement on its subject, and may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
