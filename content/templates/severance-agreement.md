about: |
  A severance agreement sets the terms on which employment ends: the final day, what the employee is paid beyond their final wages, and, usually, the employee's agreement not to bring legal claims about the employment in exchange for that extra pay. It gives both sides certainty, and done well, it lets people part on decent terms.

  This template separates what the employee is owed anyway, final pay and accrued holiday, from the severance that is conditional on signing. It covers benefits, return of property, an agreed reference and a mutual promise not to disparage, and it lists the claims that cannot be released by law, such as rights to vested pension benefits or to report wrongdoing to a regulator.

  Many laws protect employees signing a release. Some require a period to consider the agreement, a period to revoke it after signing, or independent legal advice before the release is valid. The consideration and revocation periods here follow a common US standard for older workers; adjust them, and anything else, to the law where the employee works.

# Severance Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Employer name], of [Employer address] (the "Employer"), and [Employee name] (the "Employee"), employed as [Job title].

## 1. End of employment

The Employee's employment ends on [Separation date] (the "Separation Date").

## 2. Final pay

Whether or not the Employee signs this agreement, the Employer will pay [Final pay], less required deductions, on the next regular payday after the Separation Date.

## 3. Severance

If the Employee signs this agreement and does not revoke it, the Employer will pay [Severance amount], less required deductions, [Payment timing]. In addition, [Benefits continuation].

## 4. Company property

By the Separation Date the Employee will return all company property, including [Company property], and will not keep copies of company information.

## 5. Reference

On request, the Employer will provide [Reference].

## 6. Release of claims

In exchange for the severance in section 3, the Employee releases the Employer, its affiliates and their employees from all claims arising from the employment or its ending, up to the date the Employee signs, to the fullest extent the law allows.

## 7. What is not released

This agreement does not release claims that cannot be waived by law, rights to vested retirement or pension benefits, rights under this agreement, or claims arising after signing. Nothing in it prevents the Employee from reporting possible violations of law to, or cooperating with, any government agency or regulator.

## 8. Non-disparagement

The Employee will not make false or disparaging statements about the Employer. The Employer will instruct its senior managers not to make disparaging statements about the Employee.

## 9. Time to consider and revoke

The Employee has [Consideration period] to consider this agreement and is advised to consult a lawyer before signing. After signing, the Employee may revoke it within [Revocation period] by written notice to the Employer; the release takes effect only once that period ends without revocation.

## 10. General

This agreement is the whole agreement about the ending of the employment and is governed by the law of [Governing law].
