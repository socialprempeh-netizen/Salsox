# Affiliate Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Company name] (the "Company") and [Affiliate name] (the "Affiliate").

## 1. Appointment

The Company appoints the Affiliate, on a non-exclusive basis, to refer new customers to [Product]. The Affiliate is an independent contractor and may not make commitments on the Company's behalf.

## 2. Tracking

The Company will give the Affiliate a unique referral link. A customer is credited to the Affiliate when they sign up within [Cookie days] days of last opening that link and have not used another affiliate's link since. The Company's tracking records decide which referrals are credited.

## 3. Commission

For each credited customer, the Affiliate earns [Commission], for [Commission period]. No commission is earned on taxes, on payments from the Affiliate's own accounts, or on customers the Company already had.

## 4. Payouts

Commission is held for 30 days so refunds can settle, then paid [Payout schedule], by [Payout method], once the balance reaches [Payout threshold]. Commission on a payment that is refunded or charged back is reversed. Each party is responsible for its own taxes.

## 5. Promotion rules

The Affiliate will not bid on the Company's name or trademarks in paid search, send unsolicited messages, post the link on coupon or cashback sites without written approval, or make claims about the product that the Company has not published.

## 6. Disclosure

The Affiliate will clearly disclose the affiliate relationship wherever the referral link appears, in the way the applicable advertising rules require.

## 7. Brand use

The Affiliate may use the Company's name and logo only as the Company provides them, and only to promote the product.

## 8. Term and ending

Either party may end this agreement with 14 days' written notice, or immediately for a breach of section 5 or 6. Commission earned before the end date is paid on the normal schedule, except commission from a breach.
