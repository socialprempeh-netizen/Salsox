about: |
  A non-compete agreement stops a departing employee from working for a competitor, or setting up as one, for a period after they leave. Courts and legislatures treat them with suspicion, because they limit a person's ability to earn a living. Some places ban them for most workers, others cap their length, and many require the employer to pay the employee during the restriction for it to be valid.

  This template is drafted to be as narrow as the business reason allows. It names the interest being protected, the specific activity and competitors, the area and the period, and it states what the employee is paid for accepting the restriction. It also allows the company to release the employee from the restriction early, which some laws require to be done with notice.

  Before using it, check the rules where the employee works: the maximum period, whether payment is required and at what level, and whether the employee's salary or role makes a non-compete lawful at all. A narrow confidentiality or non-solicitation agreement often protects the same interest with far less risk.

# Non-Compete Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Company name], of [Company address] (the "Company"), and [Employee name] (the "Employee"), employed as [Job title].

## 1. Why this restriction exists

In this role the Employee has access to [Business interest]. The restriction below is limited to what is needed to protect that interest.

## 2. The restriction

For [Restricted period], the Employee will not, within [Restricted area], directly or indirectly carry on [Restricted activity], whether as an employee, contractor, consultant, director or owner, for themselves or for any of the following: [Named competitors].

Nothing in this agreement stops the Employee from working in any other role or industry, from holding up to 3% of the shares of a listed company, or from doing anything the restriction does not describe.

## 3. Compensation

In return for accepting the restriction, the Company will pay the Employee [Compensation]. If the Company does not pay, the restriction ends.

## 4. Release

The Company may release the Employee from the restriction at any time by written notice. Any notice period required by the governing law applies, and payment under section 3 continues until the release takes effect.

## 5. Informing a new employer

During the restricted period, the Employee will tell any prospective employer in the restricted activity about this agreement before accepting an offer.

## 6. Reasonableness

Both parties consider the restriction reasonable. If a court finds any part of it wider than the law allows, the parties intend it to apply in the narrower form the court will enforce.

## 7. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of [Governing law].
