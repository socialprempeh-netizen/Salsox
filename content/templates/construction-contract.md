about: |
  Building work is where informal agreements cost the most. A construction contract should fix what is being built (by reference to drawings and a specification), for what price, paid when, finished by when, and what happens when something changes or goes wrong.

  This template is built around stage payments tied to physical milestones, so the owner pays for progress they can see, and the contractor's cash flow follows the work. A small retention is held through the defects period. Variations (the source of most building disputes) must be agreed in writing, with a price, before the work is done; the change-order template is made for that. Delay damages are stated and capped, and extensions of time are allowed for causes outside the contractor's control.

  It also covers site safety, insurance and permits. For large projects, standard industry forms are often used; this is for smaller works. Both parties initial the payment schedule and variations clause.

## 3. Time

Work starts on [Start date] and the Contractor will reach practical completion by [Completion date]. If the Contractor is late for reasons within its control, it pays [Delay damages]. The completion date is extended for delays caused by the Owner, variations, or events outside the Contractor's control.

## 4. Variations

No change to the Works is made without a written change order signed by both parties, stating the change, its price and any effect on the completion date.

## 5. Permits, site and safety

The Owner obtains planning and building permits. The Contractor keeps the site safe and secure, follows health and safety law, and removes its waste.

## 6. Insurance

The Contractor holds contractor's all-risk and public liability insurance for the Works and gives the Owner proof before starting.

## 7. Defects

The Contractor will make good any defects that appear during [Defects period], at its own cost.

## 8. Ending the contract and law

Either party may end this Contract if the other seriously breaches it and does not remedy it within 14 days of written notice; the Owner then pays for work properly done. This Contract is governed by [Governing law].
