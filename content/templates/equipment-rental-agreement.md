# Equipment Rental Agreement

This Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Owner name]** (the "Owner") and **[Renter name]** (the "Renter").

## 1. Equipment and condition

The Owner rents the Renter [Equipment] (the "Equipment"). At handover the Equipment is in this condition: [Condition].

Renter initials: [[initials:renter]]

## 2. Rental period and place

The rental runs from [Start date] to [Return date]. The Equipment will be used only at [Site]. [Delivery]

## 3. Fees and deposit

The rental fee is [Rental fee]. Late return is charged at [Late return fee]. The Renter pays a deposit of [Deposit], returned within seven days of the Equipment's return, less the cost of any damage, cleaning or missing parts.

## 4. Use

The Renter will use the Equipment carefully, for its intended purpose, by trained operators only, and will follow the manufacturer's instructions. The Renter will not lend, sublet or move the Equipment from the site.

## 5. Damage and loss

The Renter is responsible for damage beyond normal wear and for loss or theft while the Equipment is in its care, up to a replacement value of [Replacement value], and will report any fault or incident at once.

Renter initials: [[initials:renter]]

## 6. Return

The Renter will return the Equipment clean and in the condition recorded above. The Owner will inspect it on return in the Renter's presence where possible.

## 7. General

This Agreement is governed by [Governing law] and may be signed electronically. If the Renter needs the Equipment for longer, it will ask the Owner before the return date; an extension applies only once the Owner confirms it in writing.
