# Content Writing Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Writer name] (the "Writer") and [Client name] (the "Client").

## 1. Deliverables

The Writer will provide [Deliverables], each of [Word count] (the "Content").

## 2. Briefs and deadlines

The Client will provide a written brief for each piece, with the topic, audience, keywords and any sources. The Writer will deliver a draft within [Turnaround].

## 3. Revisions

Each piece includes [Revision rounds] rounds of revisions, requested within 7 days of delivery. A piece with no revision request in that time is treated as approved.

## 4. Changes to a brief

If the Client changes a brief after writing has started, the Writer may charge for the work already done or treat the change as a new brief.

## 5. Originality

The Content will be the Writer's original work, will not copy others' material without credit, and will be checked for accuracy against the sources given. Any use of AI writing tools will be agreed with the Client in advance and disclosed.

## 6. Rights

On payment in full, the Writer assigns copyright in the Content to the Client. The Content will be [Byline]. The Writer may show published pieces in a portfolio unless the Client asks in writing that they not.

## 7. Fees and payment

The fee is [Rate]. Invoices are due within [Payment days] days. Late invoices carry interest at the statutory rate.

## 8. Confidentiality

The Writer will keep the Client's non-public information confidential and use it only to write the Content.

## 9. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement with 14 days' written notice. Work delivered before the end is paid for, and pieces in progress are paid in proportion to the work done.
