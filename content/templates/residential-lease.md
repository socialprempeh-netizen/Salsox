# Residential Lease Agreement

This Lease is made on [Date] between **[Landlord full name or company]**, of [Landlord address] (the "Landlord"), and **[Tenant full name(s)]** (the "Tenant"). If there is more than one Tenant, each is jointly and individually responsible for all obligations under this Lease.

## 1. Property

The Landlord rents to the Tenant the property at **[Property address]** (the "Property"), including [Furniture, appliances, parking or storage included].

## 2. Term

The Lease begins on [Start date] and ends on [End date]. [Describe what happens at the end, e.g. "It then continues month to month until either party gives notice under clause 12."]

## 3. Rent

Rent is [Currency and amount] per month, payable in advance on the [Day] of each month by [Payment method]. If rent is more than [Number] days late, a late fee of [Amount] applies, where permitted by law.

## 4. Deposit

The Tenant will pay a security deposit of [Currency and amount] on signing. It will be held [Describe how, e.g. in a government-approved deposit scheme] and returned within [Number] days after the Lease ends, minus any amounts for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear, with an itemised list of deductions.

## 5. Utilities

The Tenant is responsible for [List utilities, e.g. electricity, water, internet]. The Landlord is responsible for [List utilities or charges the Landlord pays].

## 6. Use and occupants

The Property will be used only as a private home for the Tenant and [Names of other occupants]. The Tenant may not sublet or assign the Lease without the Landlord's written consent.

## 7. Condition and inventory

An inventory and condition report, signed by both parties, is attached. The Tenant will keep the Property clean and in good condition and return it in the same condition, allowing for normal wear and tear.

## 8. Repairs

The Landlord will keep the structure, plumbing, heating and electrical systems in good repair. The Tenant will report problems promptly and pay for damage caused by the Tenant or their guests.

## 9. Access

The Landlord may enter the Property for inspections or repairs with at least [Number] hours' notice, except in an emergency.

## 10. Rules

[List any rules, e.g. pets, smoking, noise, alterations, waste collection.]

## 11. Insurance

The Landlord insures the building. The Tenant is advised to insure their own belongings.

## 12. Ending the Lease

Either party may end the Lease at or after the end of the term by giving [Notice period] written notice, or as otherwise permitted by law. The Tenant will return all keys on leaving.

## 13. Governing law

This Lease is governed by the laws of [Country, state or city]. If any part is invalid under local law, the rest remains in effect.
