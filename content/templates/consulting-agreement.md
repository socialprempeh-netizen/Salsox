# Consulting Agreement

This Consulting Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Consultant name]** (the "Consultant") and **[Client legal name]** (the "Client").

## 1. Engagement

The Client engages the Consultant for [Engagement] (the "Engagement"), from [Start date] to [End date]. The Consultant will provide [Deliverables].

## 2. Independence

The Consultant is an independent contractor, decides how the Engagement is carried out, and is responsible for their own taxes and insurance.

## 3. Fees and expenses

Fees are charged on the basis of [Fee basis], and will not exceed [Fee cap]. The Client reimburses [Expenses]. The Consultant invoices monthly and the Client pays within [Payment days] days.

## 4. Information and decisions

The Consultant relies on information the Client provides and is not responsible for errors caused by incomplete or inaccurate information. Decisions taken on the Consultant's advice remain the Client's.

## 5. Ownership

Reports and materials made for the Client belong to the Client once paid for. The Consultant keeps its methods, templates and know-how.

## 6. Confidentiality and non-solicitation

Each party keeps the other's confidential information private during and after the Engagement. Neither party will hire the other's staff who worked on the Engagement during it and for [Non-solicit months] months afterwards, without the other's consent.

## 7. Ending the Engagement

Either party may end this Agreement with [Notice days] days' written notice. The Client pays for work done and expenses incurred up to the end date.

## 8. General

This Agreement is governed by [Governing law] and may be signed electronically.
