  Roofing quotes are notoriously hard to compare. One includes tear-off, another lays over the old shingles; one replaces flashing, another reuses it; and the real price often depends on rotten decking that no one can see until the old roof is off. This estimate puts each of those in writing.

  The base price covers the defined work. Decking repairs are priced per sheet, so the homeowner knows the cost of the unknown in advance, and the contractor photographs any damaged decking before replacing it. The estimate names the shingle line and colour, the underlayment and the flashing work, and states both the contractor's workmanship warranty and the manufacturer's, with who registers it.

  It also deals with the property: protection of gardens and siding, a magnetic sweep for nails, and removal of all debris. Signing the estimate accepts it and books the job.
