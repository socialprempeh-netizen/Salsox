# Reseller Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Supplier name] (the "Supplier") and [Reseller name] (the "Reseller").

## 1. Appointment

The Supplier appoints the Reseller as a [Exclusivity] reseller of [Products] (the "Products") in [Territory] (the "Territory"). The Reseller buys and resells in its own name and is not the Supplier's agent.

## 2. Prices and orders

The Reseller buys the Products at [Discount]. The Reseller will place orders of at least [Minimum order]. The Supplier may change its price list with 60 days' written notice; orders accepted before then keep the old price.

## 3. Resale prices

The Reseller sets its own resale prices. Any price the Supplier publishes is a recommendation only.

## 4. Payment and delivery

Invoices are payable within [Payment terms]. Late amounts carry interest at the rate allowed by law. Risk in the Products passes on delivery; ownership passes on payment in full.

## 5. Brand use

The Reseller may use the Supplier's trademarks only to market the Products, only in the form the Supplier provides, and will not make claims about the Products that the Supplier has not published.

## 6. Support and warranty

The Reseller provides first-line support to its customers. Warranty claims are passed to the Supplier, who will repair or replace defective Products under its standard warranty.

## 7. Reporting

Each quarter, the Reseller will report units sold and stock on hand.

## 8. Term and ending

This agreement runs for [Term] and renews for one year at a time unless either party gives 90 days' notice. Either party may end it at once if the other materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 30 days. On ending, the Reseller may sell remaining stock for 90 days.

## 9. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. The Supplier's total liability is limited to the amount the Reseller paid in the previous 12 months.
