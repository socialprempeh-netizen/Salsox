# Loan Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between:

**Lender:** [Lender name], of [Lender address]
**Borrower:** [Borrower name], of [Borrower address]

## 1. The loan

The lender agrees to lend the borrower [Loan amount] (the "Loan") for [Loan purpose]. The lender will pay the Loan to the borrower within five business days of this agreement being signed by both parties.

## 2. Interest

Interest is charged at [Interest rate]. No other charges apply except those set out in this agreement.

## 3. Repayment

The borrower will repay the Loan with interest in [Repayment schedule], by [Payment method]. Each payment is applied first to any late fees, then to interest, then to the amount borrowed.

## 4. Early repayment

The borrower may repay all or part of the Loan early at any time without penalty. Interest stops on any amount repaid early.

## 5. Late payment

If an instalment is not paid when due, the borrower will pay a late fee of [Late fee]. The late fee does not replace the borrower's duty to pay the instalment.

## 6. Default

The borrower is in default if an instalment remains unpaid [Grace period] after its due date, if the borrower becomes insolvent, or if anything the borrower stated to obtain the Loan was materially untrue. On default, the lender may give written notice that the whole outstanding balance, with interest to the date of payment, is due immediately, and may recover it together with reasonable costs of collection.

Security for the Loan: [Security].

## 8. General

This agreement is the whole agreement about the Loan and can only be changed in writing signed by both parties. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
