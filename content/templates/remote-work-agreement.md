  The data-security section is the one auditors and clients ask about: work only on company devices, no shared or public screens for client data, a locked screen when away, and an immediate report if a device is lost. # Remote Work Agreement

This Remote Work Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Employer name]** (the "Employer") and **[Employee full name]**, [Job title] (the "Employee"). It varies the Employee's employment contract as set out below; every other term of that contract continues to apply.

## 1. Arrangement

The Employee will work [Remote arrangement]. The Employer may ask the Employee to attend in person for meetings, training or client work with reasonable notice.

## 2. Availability

During remote days the Employee will be available during core hours of [Core hours] and will respond to messages and calls [Response time]. Working hours, breaks and overtime rules are those in the employment contract.

## 3. Workspace and equipment

The Employee will keep a safe, suitable workspace. The Employer will provide [Equipment provided], which remains the Employer's property and must be returned when this agreement or the employment ends. The Employer will pay [Monthly allowance].

## 4. Data security

The Employee will:

- use only Employer-provided devices and approved accounts for work;
- not let anyone else use those devices or see client information on screen;
- lock the device whenever it is unattended, and keep software up to date; and
- report a lost or stolen device, or any suspected breach, immediately.

## 5. Health and safety

The Employee will report any work-related injury or problem with their workspace, and will take part in any home-workstation assessment the Employer arranges.

## 6. Review and change

The arrangement will be reviewed after [Review period]. After that, either party may end or change it with [Notice to change] written notice, unless the law gives the Employee a right to continue.
