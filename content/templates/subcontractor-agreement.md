# Subcontractor Agreement

This Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Contractor name]** (the "Contractor") and **[Subcontractor name]** (the "Subcontractor").

## 1. Subcontract works

The Subcontractor will carry out [Subcontract works] (the "Subcontract Works") on the [Project] (the "Project"), to the standard of workmanship and materials the Contractor's main contract requires.

## 2. Price and payment

The subcontract price is [Subcontract price]. Payment is by [Payment basis]. Retention is released when the Subcontractor's defects are made good at the end of the defects period.

## 3. Programme

The Subcontractor will start on [Start date] and finish by [Finish date], coordinating with other trades as the Contractor directs.

Subcontractor initials: [[initials:subcontractor]]

## 4. Variations

The Subcontractor carries out changes only on the Contractor's written instruction, which states the price or how it will be valued. Unauthorised extras will not be paid.

## 5. Site rules and safety

The Subcontractor will follow the Contractor's site rules and safety plan, work during [Site hours], provide trained and equipped workers, and keep its work areas clean.

## 6. Insurance

The Subcontractor will hold [Insurance] and give proof before starting.

## 7. Defects and flow-down

The Subcontractor will make good defects in the Subcontract Works during [Defects period], and accepts the obligations of the main contract that apply to the Subcontract Works.

## 8. Subcontracting, termination and law

The Subcontractor may not subcontract any part of the work without the Contractor's written consent. The Contractor may end this Agreement for serious breach not remedied within seven days of notice. This Agreement is governed by [Governing law].
