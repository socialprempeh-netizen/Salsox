about: |
  A maintenance agreement replaces one-off call-outs with a planned arrangement: the provider services the equipment on a schedule and responds to breakdowns within an agreed time, for a fixed fee. For the client, it keeps equipment running and makes the cost predictable; for the provider, it is recurring work.

  This template lists the equipment covered, the planned visits, the response time for faults, what parts and labour the fee includes, and the exclusions that are charged separately, such as damage, misuse and major component replacement. It also sets the client's duties, such as safe access and reporting faults promptly.

  Attach a schedule listing each item with its make, model, serial number and location, so there is no doubt about what is covered.

# Maintenance Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Provider name] (the "Provider") and [Client name] (the "Client").

## 1. Equipment and site

The Provider will maintain [Equipment] (the "Equipment") at [Site address].

## 2. Planned maintenance

The Provider will make [Visit frequency] to inspect, clean, test and service the Equipment to the manufacturer's recommendations, and will give the Client a written report after each visit.

## 3. Breakdowns

The Client may report a fault at any time. The Provider will attend [Response time] and will use reasonable efforts to fix the fault on the first visit.

## 4. Fee and what it covers

The fee is [Annual fee]. It covers labour for planned visits and breakdown call-outs during working hours, and [Included parts]. Other parts are supplied at the Provider's list price, with the Client's approval before fitting. Call-outs outside working hours are charged at the Provider's published rates.

## 5. Exclusions

The fee does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, power failure or work by others; equipment not in schedule 1; replacing compressors, motors or other major components; or changes required by new regulations. The Provider will quote for these separately.

## 6. The Client's duties

The Client will give safe access to the Equipment, report faults promptly, and not allow anyone else to work on the Equipment during this agreement without telling the Provider.

## 7. Term and renewal

This agreement starts on [Start date] and runs for one year. It renews for a further year unless either party gives 60 days' written notice. The Provider may change the fee at renewal by giving 60 days' notice.

## 8. Liability

The Provider is liable for loss caused by its negligence, up to the annual fee, and not for indirect loss or loss of production.
