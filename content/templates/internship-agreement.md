  An internship works when both sides agree on what it is for. This agreement writes that down: the dates, the hours, the stipend, who supervises, and three concrete learning goals the intern should reach by the end.

  The learning goals do two jobs. They make the placement worth the intern's time, and in many countries they are part of what distinguishes a genuine internship from unpaid work, which labour law treats very differently. If the intern will mostly do productive work for the business, pay at least the minimum wage.

  The template also covers conduct, confidentiality and who owns the work, and lets either side end the placement early with short notice. If the internship is part of a course, add the school as a third signer.

# Internship Agreement

This Internship Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between **[Organisation name]**, of [Organisation address] (the "Organisation"), and **[Intern full name]**, of [Intern address] (the "Intern").

## 1. Internship details

The Intern will join the Organisation as a [Internship title] intern in the [Team name] team, from [Start date] to [End date], working at [Work location].

## 2. Learning goals

By the end of the internship, the Intern should be able to:

- [First learning goal];
- [Second learning goal]; and
- [Third learning goal].

## 3. Supervision

[Supervisor name], [Supervisor title], will supervise the Intern, set tasks, give regular feedback and hold a review at the midpoint and at the end.

## 4. Hours, stipend and expenses

The Intern will work [Weekly hours] hours per week, with reasonable time off for exams or course commitments given notice. The Intern will receive [Stipend], less any deductions required by law. [Expenses]

## 5. Conduct and confidentiality

The Intern will follow the Organisation's reasonable instructions and policies, keep its confidential information secret during and after the internship, and return all materials and equipment when it ends.

## 6. Work produced

Work the Intern produces belongs to the Organisation, to the extent allowed by law. With approval, the Intern may describe it in a portfolio or CV without disclosing confidential information.

## 7. Ending early and reference

Either party may end the internship early with [Notice period] written notice. At the end, the Organisation will provide a written reference or certificate of completion on request.
