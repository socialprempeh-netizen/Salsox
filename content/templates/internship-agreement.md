# Internship Agreement

This Internship Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Date] between **[Organisation legal name]**, of [Organisation address] (the "Organisation"), and **[Intern full name]**, of [Intern address] (the "Intern").

## 1. Internship details

The Intern will join the Organisation as a [Internship title] intern in the [Team or department name] team. The internship runs from [Start date] to [End date], at [Work location or "remotely"].

## 2. Purpose and learning goals

The internship is intended to give the Intern practical experience in [Field]. By the end of the internship, the Intern should have:

- [Learning goal 1];
- [Learning goal 2]; and
- [Learning goal 3].

## 3. Supervision

[Supervisor name], [Supervisor title], will supervise the Intern, set tasks, give regular feedback and hold a review at [Midpoint and end, or other schedule].

## 4. Hours

The Intern will work [Number] hours per week, normally [Days and times]. The Organisation will allow reasonable time off for exams or course commitments with advance notice.

## 5. Stipend and expenses

The Intern will receive [Currency and amount] per [Week or month], paid [Pay frequency], less any deductions required by law. [Describe expenses covered, e.g. travel, equipment, or "No expenses are covered."]

## 6. Conduct and policies

The Intern will follow the Organisation's reasonable instructions and policies, including those on health and safety, data protection and acceptable use of equipment.

## 7. Confidentiality

The Intern will keep the Organisation's confidential information secret during and after the internship, use it only for their internship tasks, and return all materials and equipment when it ends.

## 8. Work produced

Work the Intern produces during the internship belongs to the Organisation, to the extent allowed by law. With the Organisation's approval, the Intern may describe their work in a portfolio or CV without disclosing confidential information.

## 9. Ending early

Either party may end the internship early by giving [Notice period] written notice.

## 10. Reference

At the end of the internship, the Organisation will provide [A written reference or certificate of completion] on request.
