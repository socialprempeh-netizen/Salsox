**Service provider:** [Business name]

Legal name: [Client legal name]
Registration number: [Registration number]

## 2. Contacts

Main contact: [Main contact]
Billing contact: [Billing contact]
Preferred way to communicate: [Preferred channel]

## 3. Engagement

Services agreed: [Services agreed]
Start date: [Start date]

What the client wants to achieve: [Client goals]

## 4. Access

To deliver the services, the client will provide [Systems access]. Access will be used only for the services and removed when the engagement ends.

## 5. Consent

The client confirms that:

- the details above are correct, and it will tell the provider of any change;
- it has read and accepts the provider's terms of service, which govern the engagement; and
- it has read the provider's privacy notice and agrees to the provider processing the information on this form, and information shared during the engagement, to deliver the services.
