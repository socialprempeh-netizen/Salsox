# Bookkeeping Services Agreement

This agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Bookkeeper name] (the "Bookkeeper") and [Client name] (the "Client").

## 1. Services

From [Start date], the Bookkeeper will provide [Services] (the "Services"). Any other work, such as year-end accounts, tax advice or catch-up of earlier periods, is quoted separately.

## 2. Software

The books are kept in [Software]. The Client gives the Bookkeeper user access, and keeps administrator control of the account.

## 3. The Client's records

The Client will supply bank statements, receipts, invoices and payroll information by [Records deadline]. If records arrive late, the reporting deadline moves by the same number of days, and extra work caused by late or incomplete records is charged at the hourly rate.

## 4. Responsibility

The Bookkeeper will carry out the Services with reasonable skill and care. The Client is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the records it supplies, and for reviewing, approving and filing any return the Bookkeeper prepares. The Bookkeeper does not audit the records and does not give tax or legal advice.

## 5. Reports

The Bookkeeper will provide [Reports].

## 6. Fees

The fee is [Monthly fee], payable within 14 days. Additional work is charged at GBP 45 an hour, agreed before it starts. The fee may be reviewed once a year with 30 days' notice.

## 7. Confidentiality and data protection

The Bookkeeper will keep the Client's information confidential, will process personal data only on the Client's instructions and to provide the Services, will keep it secure, and will delete or return it when this agreement ends.

## 8. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice. Once outstanding fees are paid, the Bookkeeper will remove its access, return any original documents, and give the incoming accountant reasonable handover information.
