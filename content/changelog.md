## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-01

🎉 **First release.** Send a PDF, get it signed from any phone, and get paid in the same step.

### Added
- **Quick Send**: upload a PDF, enter the signers' emails, and it goes out with a signature and a date field placed for each of them
- **Full document setup**: signers, approvers, viewers and CC recipients, fields placed on the page, signing in parallel or in a set order, and a link expiry of your choice
- **Mobile-first signing**: one field at a time, a next-step bar that stays in reach, inputs sized for thumbs, and a signature you can draw, type or upload and reuse
- **Sign & Pay**: ask for a payment as part of signing, through Stripe or Paystack, with the payout going straight to your connected account
- **Share links any way you like**: every signing link can be copied, or sent over WhatsApp or SMS, as well as by email
- **Correct or renew without starting over**: fix a recipient's address and the old link stops working at once, or extend an expired document and the links already shared work again
- **Sealed copies**: a completed document gets a certificate page with every recipient, the full audit trail and file hashes, and the original is never modified
- **Reminders and expiry** handled for you, every day
- **Export everything**: one ZIP with every original, every sealed PDF and every audit trail, on any plan and even after you cancel

### Billing
- Unlimited sending on every plan, with fair-use limits that only exist to stop spam
- Cancel, or undo a cancellation, from the app itself
- Your renewal date on the billing page, and an email before every renewal: 7 days ahead on monthly plans, 14 on yearly ones
