---
title: "Sign & Pay: get paid as you get signed"
summary: "Connect Stripe or Paystack on Payouts, add a payment to a document, and see what the payer does."
minutes: 3
---

## Before you record

- Sign in as **Ama Mensah** (**ama.mensah@example.com**) on the **Business** plan.
- Use a test-mode Stripe account, or a Paystack test account, already connected and showing **Ready**. Never show real bank or card details.
- Have a draft on the **Send** step with signer **Kofi Boateng** (kofi.boateng@example.com).
- For scene 4, a phone or 390 × 844 window with Kofi's signing link, and a test card.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** the dashboard.

**Say:** "With Sign & Pay, your client pays a deposit or an invoice in the same step as signing. It's part of the Business plan."

### Scene 2: Connect a payout account

**Do:** select **Payouts**.

**Say:** "First, tell us where the money goes. On Payouts, connect Stripe, Paystack, or both."

**Do:** point to the Stripe card, then the Paystack card.

**Say:** "Stripe takes cards in more than forty countries: choose your country and finish setup on Stripe's secure page. Paystack takes cards and mobile money in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa: enter your business name, bank and account number. When it says Ready, you're set."

### Scene 3: Add a payment

**Do:** open the draft and go to the **Send** step. Tick **Collect a payment (Sign & Pay)**.

**Say:** "On the Send step, tick Collect a payment."

**Do:** type **250.00**, choose **GHS** (or **USD**), and choose **Kofi Boateng** under **Who pays**.

**Say:** "Enter the amount, choose the currency, and who pays."

**Do:** select **Send for signature**.

**Say:** "Then send as usual."

### Scene 4: The payer's side

**On screen:** Kofi's signing link on the phone.

**Do:** sign the fields, then show the last button reading **Pay … and finish**.

**Say:** "Kofi signs as normal. The last button asks him to pay. He pays by card or, with Paystack, mobile money, and his signature and payment arrive together."

### Scene 5: Close

**On screen:** the document page, with **Paid** on Kofi's card.

**Say:** "Back on the document, Kofi's card shows Paid. You'll find a receipt under Billing, in Payments and receipts. The provider's processing fee comes out at cost, and we take no cut."
