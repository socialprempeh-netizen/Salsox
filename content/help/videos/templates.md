## Before you record

- Sign in as **Ama Mensah** (**ama.mensah@example.com**) on the **Personal** plan or above, so **Save as your own template** is available.
- No saved templates yet.
- Signer for scene 4: **Kofi Boateng**, **kofi.boateng@example.com**.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** the dashboard.

**Say:** "Templates are ready-made documents with every signature field already in place. Let's use one, and save it for next time."

### Scene 2: Find a template

**Do:** select **Templates** in the menu.

**Say:** "Go to Templates."

**Do:** type **NDA** in the search box, then open **Mutual NDA** (or the first NDA result).

**Say:** "Search, or filter by category or industry. I'll open a non-disclosure agreement."

### Scene 3: Fill in the blanks

**Do:** select **Use sample details**.

**Say:** "Under Fill in the details, every blank is listed. Use sample details shows how it reads with example text."

**Do:** replace the company name with **Mensah Design Studio**.

**Say:** "Type your own details. Anything you leave empty prints as a line to fill in by hand."

### Scene 4: Save it as your own

**Do:** open **Save as your own template**, type **Standard client NDA** and select **Save template**.

**Say:** "To reuse these details, open Save as your own template, give it a name, and save. Saving templates is part of the Personal and Business plans."

### Scene 5: Create the document

**Do:** select **Create document and open the editor**.

**Say:** "Now create the document. It opens in the editor with its fields already placed. Nothing is sent yet."

**Do:** on **Recipients**, type **Kofi Boateng** and **kofi.boateng@example.com**.

**Say:** "Add your signer's name and email, then continue to Send as usual."

### Scene 6: Use it again

**Do:** go back to **Templates**.

**Say:** "Next time, your template is at the top under Your saved templates. Open it, change what's different, and send."
