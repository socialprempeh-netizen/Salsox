title: "Plans, billing and your account"
summary: "Compare plans, upgrade, manage or cancel your subscription, export your documents, and update your account details."
minutes: 3
---

## Before you record

- Sign in as **Ama Mensah** (**ama.mensah@example.com**) on the free plan, with Stripe in test mode.
- Use a Stripe test card for checkout. Never show real card details.
- Have at least two sent documents, so the export has something in it.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** the dashboard.

**Say:** "Here's how plans and billing work, and where to manage your account."

### Scene 2: Compare plans

**Do:** select **Billing**, then scroll to **Available plans**.

**Say:** "Go to Billing. Under Available plans you can compare them. Free sends three documents a month. Personal is unlimited, and lets you save your own templates. Business adds Sign & Pay, signing order and approvers, and a certificate page in every signed PDF."

**Do:** switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly**.

**Say:** "Yearly billing gives you two months free."

### Scene 3: Upgrade

**Do:** select **Upgrade to Personal**, complete checkout with the test card, and return.

**Say:** "Select Upgrade, pay on the secure checkout page, and you're back on Billing with your new plan, usually within a few seconds."

### Scene 4: Manage or cancel

**Do:** point to **Manage Billing**, then to **Cancel subscription**.

**Say:** "Manage Billing opens the billing portal, to switch plans or update your card. Cancel subscription stops renewal: you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. We always email you before a renewal."

### Scene 5: Export

**Do:** scroll to **Export all documents** and select **Download ZIP**.

**Say:** "Your documents are yours. Download ZIP gives you every original, every signed PDF and every audit trail. It's free on every plan, even after you cancel."

### Scene 6: Account settings

**Do:** select **Settings**.

**Say:** "Finally, Settings. Change your name, your sign-in email, or your password here, and turn on two-factor authentication for extra security."
