## Before you record

- Sign in as **Ama Mensah** (**ama.mensah@example.com**).
- Prepare: a sent document for **Kofi Boateng** (kofi.boateng@example.com); a second sent document with a typo, **kofi.boateng@exmple.com**; an **Expired** document; a PDF over 4 MB on the desktop; and a draft on the **Fields** step.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** **Documents**.

**Say:** "Here are the five things people ask us about most, and how to fix each one in under a minute."

### Scene 2: A signer didn't get the email

**Do:** open Kofi's document and point to his status, then to **Resend**, then select **Share link**.

**Say:** "First: a signer says they didn't get the email. Check their status here. Ask them to look in spam, select Resend, or skip email altogether: Share link sends it by WhatsApp or SMS."

### Scene 3: Wrong email address

**Do:** open the document with the typo, select **Edit**, correct the address to **kofi.boateng@example.com** and select **Save changes**.

**Say:** "Second: you typed the wrong email. No need to start again. Select Edit, fix it, and save. The old link stops working and the right address gets a new one. Their fields stay where they were."

### Scene 4: An expired link

**Do:** open the expired document and select **Renew and resend**.

**Say:** "Third: the link expired. Select Renew and resend. Everyone who still has to sign gets a fresh link, and anything already signed is kept."

### Scene 5: A PDF won't upload

**Do:** go to **New document** and choose the large PDF, so **This file is larger than 4 MB.** appears.

**Say:** "Fourth: a PDF won't upload. Files can be up to four megabytes. Big files are usually scans: compress it, or scan again at a lower resolution, and try again. If it isn't a PDF at all, save it as a PDF first."

### Scene 6: A field won't place

**Do:** open the draft on **Fields**, select a signer and **Signature**, and tap the page.

**Say:** "And fifth: placing fields. Check the right person is selected, choose the field type, then tap the page itself, below the toolbar. Every signer needs at least one field before you can send."

**Do:** add `?placementDebug=1` to the address and press Enter; show the **Placement diagnostics** box.

**Say:** "If a field still won't place, add question mark placement debug equals one to the address. Tap once, screenshot this box, and send it to us. It shows exactly where your tap went."

### Scene 7: Close

**Say:** "For everything else, the Help Center has a step-by-step article. And if you're still stuck, contact us."
