---
title: "Getting started: your account and first PDF"
summary: "Create an account, confirm your email and upload your first PDF, ready to add signers."
minutes: 2
---

## Before you record

- Use a fresh browser window at 1280 × 800, signed out.
- Have a sample PDF ready on the desktop, named **Service agreement.pdf**, under 4 MB.
- Use the fictional sender **Ama Mensah**, **ama.mensah@example.com**. Never show a real inbox or real customer data.
- Have the confirmation email open in a second tab, ready to switch to.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** the home page.

**Say:** "Hi, I'm going to show you how to create your account and upload your first document. It takes about two minutes."

### Scene 2: Sign up

**Do:** select **Get started** in the top right.

**Say:** "Select Get started. You can continue with Google, or use your email address and a password."

**Do:** type **Ama Mensah** in the name field, **ama.mensah@example.com** in the email field, and a password.

**Say:** "I'll use my email. Your name is optional, and your password needs at least eight characters."

**Do:** select **Create account**.

**Say:** "Then select Create account."

### Scene 3: Confirm your email

**Do:** switch to the tab with the confirmation email and select its link.

**Say:** "We send you a confirmation email. Open it and select the link. That confirms your address and signs you in. You can prepare documents before you do this, but you need to confirm before you can send one."

### Scene 4: Upload a PDF

**On screen:** the dashboard.

**Do:** select **Documents** in the menu, then **New document**.

**Say:** "Now let's upload a document. Go to Documents, then New document."

**Do:** select **Tap to choose a PDF, or drop it here** and choose **Service agreement.pdf**.

**Say:** "Choose your PDF. It can be up to four megabytes. On a computer you can also drag it straight onto this box."

**Do:** point to the **Title** field without typing.

**Say:** "The title is optional. If you leave it empty, we use the file name."

**Do:** select **Continue to recipients**.

**Say:** "Select Continue to recipients, and your document opens in the editor."

### Scene 5: Close

**On screen:** the editor's Recipients step.

**Say:** "That's it: your account is ready and your first document is uploaded. Your original file is never changed. In the next video, we'll add the people who need to sign it and send it."
