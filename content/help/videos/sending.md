minutes: 4
---

## Before you record

- Sign in as the fictional sender **Ama Mensah** (**ama.mensah@example.com**), on a confirmed account with documents left this month.
- Have **Service agreement.pdf** ready on the desktop.
- Signers: **Kofi Boateng**, **kofi.boateng@example.com**, and **Efua Mensah**, **efua.mensah@example.com**.
- Window at 1280 × 800.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** the dashboard.

**Say:** "There are two ways to send a document for signature. Quick Send, for when people just need to sign, and the full editor, for when you want control over every field. I'll show you both."

### Scene 2: Quick Send

**Do:** select **Quick Send** in the menu.

**Say:** "Start with Quick Send."

**Do:** choose **Service agreement.pdf**.

**Say:** "Choose your PDF."

**Do:** type **kofi.boateng@example.com** in **Who needs to sign?**

**Say:** "Type the email address of each person who needs to sign. For more than one, separate them with commas."

**Do:** open **Title, message and expiry** and point to the three fields.

**Say:** "Here you can change the title, add a message, and choose when the link expires. All optional."

**Do:** select **Send to 1 signer**.

**Say:** "Select Send. We place a signature and a date for each signer at the end of the document and email them their link."

### Scene 3: The document page

**On screen:** the document page.

**Say:** "You land on the document's page. Under Recipients you can see Kofi's status: Sent, then Opened, then Signed. Reminders go out automatically every three days, and Send reminder sends one now."

### Scene 4: The full editor, recipients

**Do:** select **Documents**, then **New document**, upload the PDF and select **Continue to recipients**.

**Say:** "Now the full editor. Upload the PDF and continue to recipients."

**Do:** type **Kofi Boateng** and **kofi.boateng@example.com**.

**Say:** "Add each person's name and email. A phone number is optional: it lets you share their link by WhatsApp or SMS later."

**Do:** open the **Role** list.

**Say:** "Choose what each person does. Signs, Approves, Views, or Gets a copy at the end."

**Do:** select **Add recipient**, add **Efua Mensah**, **efua.mensah@example.com**, and set her role to **Gets a copy**.

**Say:** "Select Add recipient for each extra person. Efua gets a copy once everyone has signed."

**Do:** select **Next**.

### Scene 5: Fields (short)

**Do:** select **Signature**, then tap the page near the bottom.

**Say:** "Place at least one field for each signer. The next video covers fields in detail."

**Do:** select **Next**.

### Scene 6: Review and send

**Do:** point to **Document title**, **Email subject**, **Message to recipients** and **Signing links expire after**.

**Say:** "Check the title, add a subject and a message if you like, and choose when the links expire."

**Do:** select **Send for signature**.

**Say:** "Select Send for signature. Each person gets their own link by email."

### Scene 7: Close

**On screen:** the document page.

**Say:** "If someone doesn't see the email, select Share link next to their name to send it by WhatsApp or SMS. That's sending. Next, placing fields."
