summary: "Place, move and resize every field type, then follow a signer through signing on a phone, plus the free plan's allowance."
minutes: 4
---

## Before you record

- Sign in as **Ama Mensah** (**ama.mensah@example.com**) on the free plan, with **3 of 3 free documents left this month** showing.
- Have a draft open on the **Fields** step with two signers: **Kofi Boateng** (kofi.boateng@example.com) and **Efua Mensah** (efua.mensah@example.com).
- For scene 5, have a phone, or a browser window at 390 × 844, ready to open Kofi's signing link.

## Script

### Scene 1: Introduction

**On screen:** the editor, **Fields** step.

**Say:** "Fields are where each person signs, dates or fills something in. Here's how to place them, and what your signers see."

### Scene 2: Place a field

**Do:** select **Kofi Boateng** in the toolbar.

**Say:** "First, choose whose field it is. Each person has their own colour."

**Do:** select **Signature**.

**Say:** "Then choose the field type. The hint changes to: now tap the page where the field should go."

**Do:** click on the page above the signature line.

**Say:** "Tap or click the page, and the field appears right there."

### Scene 3: Move, resize, remove

**Do:** drag the field a little to the right.

**Say:** "Drag a field to move it."

**Do:** drag its bottom-right corner to make it wider.

**Say:** "Drag the corner to resize it."

**Do:** with the field selected, point to **Required** and **Remove**.

**Say:** "With a field selected, you can make it optional, or remove it. Select Done when you're finished."

### Scene 4: The other field types

**Do:** select **Date** and place it beside the signature; then **Text**, then **Checkbox**.

**Say:** "There are seven field types. Signature and initials. Name. Date, which fills itself in. Email. Text, for anything you need them to type. And checkbox, which starts optional so they can leave it unticked."

**Do:** select **Efua Mensah** and place a **Signature** for her.

**Say:** "Every signer needs at least one field before you can send."

### Scene 5: What the signer sees

**On screen:** Kofi's signing link on the phone.

**Say:** "Here's what Kofi sees on his phone. No account or app needed."

**Do:** tick **I agree to sign electronically…**, then select **Review and sign**.

**Say:** "He agrees to sign electronically and selects Review and sign."

**Do:** tap the signature field, choose **Draw**, sign with a finger, then select **Adopt and sign**.

**Say:** "We take him to each field in turn. He can draw, type or upload a signature."

**Do:** select **Next field**, then **Finish signing**.

**Say:** "Next field, then Finish signing. Done. Everyone gets the signed PDF once the last person signs."

### Scene 6: The free allowance

**On screen:** **Documents**, **New document**, with the allowance note visible.

**Say:** "One last thing. On the free plan you can send three documents a month; this note shows how many you have left. A document only counts when you send it, and the count resets on the first of each month. For unlimited documents, see Billing."
