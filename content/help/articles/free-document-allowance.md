A free account sends 3 documents a month. Above the upload and Quick Send forms you'll see how many are left, for example **2 of 3 free documents left this month**.

## How it's counted

1. A document counts once, **when you send it**. Uploading, preparing and saving drafts don't count.
2. It counts the same however many people sign it.
3. The count starts again on the first day of each month.
4. When none are left, the note reads **You've sent your 3 free documents this month**, and sending a new document asks you to choose a plan. Nothing is lost: your drafts stay, and documents you already sent stay open for signing.

## Get more

5. Select **See plans** in the note, or go to **Billing** in the dashboard.
6. Choose **Upgrade to Personal** for unlimited documents, or **Upgrade to Business** for unlimited documents plus Sign & Pay, signing order and sealed PDFs with an audit certificate. See [Plans and what they include](/resources/help/plans-and-what-they-include).

## Good to know

- Reminders, renewing links and correcting a recipient's email never use up your allowance.
- The free **Request a signature** tool on our website is counted separately: a free account can send one request prepared with it.
