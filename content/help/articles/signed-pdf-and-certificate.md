When the last person signs, the document is completed and sealed: the signatures and filled-in fields are drawn onto a copy of your PDF, and that signed copy is fixed so any later change can be detected.

## Get the signed PDF

1. Everyone on the document is emailed the signed PDF when it completes.
2. You can also open the document from **Documents** and, under **Files**, select **Signed PDF + certificate** (on Business) or **Signed PDF**.

## What the certificate page shows (Business plan)

3. A last page is added to the signed PDF with the document's audit trail: who signed, in which role, when, with their email and IP address.
4. It carries a **verification code** and a QR code. Anyone holding the PDF can use them to [check it's genuine](/resources/help/verify-a-signed-document).

## Good to know

- The original PDF you uploaded is never modified. You can always download it under **Files**, **Original PDF**.
- On the Free and Personal plans the signed PDF has no certificate page, but the audit trail is still kept and shown on the document's page.
