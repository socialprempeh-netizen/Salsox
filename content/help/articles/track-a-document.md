Every sent document has its own page with its status, each recipient's progress and a full audit trail.

## Check a document's progress

1. Select **Documents** in the dashboard. Each row shows how many people have signed, such as **1 of 2 signed**. Use the filters (**Awaiting**, **Completed**, **Drafts**, **Expired**) or search by title or email.
2. Open a document. Its status shows at the top, for example **Awaiting signatures**.
3. Under **Recipients**, each person shows **Sent**, **Opened**, **Signed** with the date, or **Declined**.

## Remind people

4. Reminders are automatic: anyone who hasn't signed is reminded by email every 3 days.
5. To remind everyone now, select **Send reminder** at the top of the page. To resend one person's email, select **Resend** on their card.

## Extend or cancel

6. If links are close to expiring, select **Extend links**. Anything already signed is kept.
7. To stop a document, select **Cancel document** and confirm. Links stop working; what was already signed stays in the audit trail.

When everyone has signed, the status becomes **Completed** and everyone is emailed the signed PDF.
