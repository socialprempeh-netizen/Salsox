Most missing emails are in a spam folder or went to a mistyped address. You don't have to wait to find out: you can send the link yourself.

## Fix it

1. Open the document from **Documents** and find the person under **Recipients**.
2. Check their status. **Sent** means our email provider accepted the message. **Email not delivered** means it was refused, usually because the address doesn't exist.
3. Check the address on their card. If it's wrong, select **Edit**, correct it and select **Save changes**. They get a fresh link at the new address. See [I sent it to the wrong email address](/resources/help/wrong-email-address).
4. If the address is right, ask them to look in spam, promotions or other folders.
5. Select **Resend** on their card to send the email again.
6. Or skip email: select **Share link** and send it by WhatsApp or SMS. See [Share a signing link](/resources/help/share-a-signing-link).

## Good to know

- People who haven't signed are reminded automatically every 3 days.
- Work email systems sometimes hold messages from new senders. Asking the person to check their quarantine, or sharing the link directly, gets round it.
