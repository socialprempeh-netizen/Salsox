A signer can choose **Decline to sign** instead of signing, and say why. It's better than silence: you learn exactly what's wrong.

## See why and fix it

1. You're notified when someone declines. Open the document from **Documents**.
2. Under **Recipients**, their card shows **Declined**, with their reason underneath if they gave one.
3. A declined document is closed for signing: nobody else can sign it.
4. Fix what they raised in your original file, then upload it as a new document and send it again. A [saved template](/resources/help/save-your-own-template) makes this quicker for documents you send often.

## Good to know

- Declined by mistake? The signer sees a note to ask you to send it again. Send a new document.
- The decline, and its reason, are kept in the audit trail.
