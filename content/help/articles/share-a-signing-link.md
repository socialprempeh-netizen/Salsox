Every recipient is emailed their link when you send. You can also send it yourself, which is often faster for people who live on WhatsApp.

## Share a link

1. Open the document from **Documents**.
2. In **Recipients**, find the person and select **Share link**.
3. Choose **Send via WhatsApp**, **Send via SMS**, **Send via email app** or **Copy link**.
4. Your app opens with a short message and their link filled in. Send it as usual.

## Good to know

- Each link belongs to one person. Don't send someone else's link: whoever opens it signs in that person's name.
- WhatsApp and SMS open straight to the right person when their phone number has a country code. You can add or fix it with **Edit**.
- **Share link** shows while that person still has something to do.
