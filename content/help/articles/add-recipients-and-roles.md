Everyone you add to a document is a recipient. Their role decides what they do with it.

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Signs** | Fills in their fields and signs. |
| **Approves** | Reviews the document and approves it, without being a party to it. Business plan. |
| **Views** | Can open and read the document. Nothing to sign. |
| **Gets a copy** | Receives the signed PDF once everyone has signed. |

## Add a recipient

1. On the editor's **Recipients** step, fill in **Name** and **Email**.
2. Optionally add a **Phone** number with the country code, for example +233 20 123 4567, so you can share their link by WhatsApp or SMS.
3. Choose their **Role** from the list.
4. To add another person, select **Add recipient**. A document can have up to 10 recipients.
5. To remove someone, select the bin icon on their card.

## Good to know

- At least one recipient has to sign or approve, and every signer needs at least one field.
- The colour stripe on each card is that person's colour in the **Fields** step, so you can see whose field is whose.
- To decide who goes first, see [Set a signing order](/resources/help/set-a-signing-order).
