If you send the same kind of document again and again, save your filled-in version once and only change what's different each time. Saving templates is part of the Personal and Business plans.

## Save a template

1. In **Templates**, open the template you use and fill in the blanks that stay the same, such as your business name and terms.
2. Open **Save as your own template** below the form.
3. Type a **Template name**, for example *Standard client NDA*.
4. Select **Save template**.

## Use it again

5. Go to **Templates**. Your saved ones are listed first, under **Your saved templates**.
6. Open it. Your details are already filled in: change what's different, then select **Create document and open the editor**.

## Update or delete it

7. To change it, open it, edit the details, then open **Save as your own template** and select **Update template**.
8. To remove it, select **Delete saved template** in the same place.

## Good to know

- A saved template starts from one of the templates in the library. A PDF you uploaded yourself can't be saved as a template.
- On the free plan, the save section shows **Saving your own templates is part of the Personal plan.** with a **See plans** link.
