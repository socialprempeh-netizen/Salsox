There are three plans, plus a one-time Lifetime option. Every plan includes the same signing experience for your signers, the audit trail, WhatsApp and SMS link sharing, and free export of all your documents.

| | Free | Personal | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Documents per month | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Templates library | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Save your own templates | No | Yes | Yes |
| Signing order and approvers | No | No | Yes |
| Sign & Pay | No | No | Yes |
| Certificate page with audit trail in the signed PDF | No | No | Yes |

There is also **Lifetime**: everything in Business for a single payment, with no subscription to cancel.

Current prices, monthly and yearly, are on the [pricing page](/pricing#plans). Yearly billing includes two months free.

## Choose a plan

1. In the dashboard, select **Billing**.
2. Under **Available plans**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Select **Upgrade to Personal** or **Upgrade to Business**, and pay on the secure checkout page.
4. You're returned to **Billing**. Your new plan shows under **Current Plan** within a few seconds.

To switch or cancel later, see [Upgrade, switch or cancel your plan](/resources/help/upgrade-switch-or-cancel).
