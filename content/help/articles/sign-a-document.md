Your signers don't need an account or an app. Everything happens in their browser, on a phone or a computer. You can share this page with them.

## Sign a document

1. Open the email (or the WhatsApp or SMS message) and select the signing link.
2. Read the summary: who sent it and how many fields you need to fill in.
3. Tick **I agree to sign electronically and to receive this document and related records electronically.**
4. Select **Review and sign**.
5. The page takes you to each field in turn. For a signature, choose **Draw**, **Type** or **Upload**, then select **Adopt and sign**. Dates fill themselves in.
6. Select **Next field** to move on. The counter shows how many fields are left.
7. Select **Finish signing**. If the sender asked for a payment, the button says **Pay** and the amount, and you pay before finishing.

You'll see **You've signed**. Once everyone has signed, everyone is emailed the signed PDF, and the signing page offers **Download signed PDF**.

## Don't want to sign?

Select **Decline to sign** instead, and optionally say why. The sender is told, with your reason.

## Good to know

- **Not your turn yet** means someone else signs first. You'll be emailed when it's your turn.
- **This link has expired** means the sender needs to renew it. Anything you already filled in is kept.
