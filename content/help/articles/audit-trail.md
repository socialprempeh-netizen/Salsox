Every document keeps an audit trail: a dated record of everything that happened to it. It's how you can show who signed, when, and from where.

## What it records

Each entry has the date and time, the email address of the person who did it, and their IP address. The events are:

- **Document uploaded** and **Sent for signature**
- **Opened by recipient**, each time someone opens their link
- **Field completed**, for each field filled in
- **Recipient signed** or **Recipient declined**
- **Recipient details corrected**, when you fix an email address
- **Reminder sent** and **Links renewed**
- Payments: **Payment started**, **Payment received**, **Payment failed or abandoned**, **Payment refunded**, and disputes
- **Completed by all parties**, **Cancelled by sender** or **Links expired**

## See a document's audit trail

1. Open the document from **Documents**.
2. Scroll to **Audit trail**. The newest event is at the top.
3. On the Business plan, the same trail is printed on a certificate page at the end of the signed PDF. See [The signed PDF and its certificate](/resources/help/signed-pdf-and-certificate).

## Good to know

- Entries can't be edited, by you or anyone else.
- Before they start, every signer ticks a box agreeing to sign electronically.
