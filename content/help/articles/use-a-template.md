Templates are ready-made documents for the things small businesses send most: proposals, quotes, contracts, NDAs, HR forms, leases and more. Each one comes with its signature, date and initials fields already placed.

## Use a template

1. In the dashboard, select **Templates**. Search, or filter by **Category**, **Industry**, **Team** or **Type**.
2. Select a template to open it.
3. Under **Fill in the details**, type your details into each blank. Select **Use sample details** to see how it reads with example text first. Any blank you leave empty prints as a line to fill in by hand.
4. Check the **Document title**.
5. Select **Create document and open the editor**. Nothing is sent yet.
6. In the editor, add each signer's name and email on the **Recipients** step. The fields are already placed for them.
7. Continue to **Send** and select **Send for signature**.

## Good to know

- You can also start from the public [template library](/templates): select **Use this template** and sign in or sign up; the template opens straight after.
- Templates are general starting points, not legal advice. Check that one suits your situation before you rely on it.
- To reuse your filled-in details next time, [save it as your own template](/resources/help/save-your-own-template).
