---
title: "My PDF won't upload"
description: "Fix the common upload problems: a file that isn't a PDF, a PDF over 4 MB, or a scan that's too large, with free ways to shrink it."
category: troubleshooting
order: 4
---

Uploads take PDF files up to 4 MB. The message under the upload box tells you which limit you hit.

## "Please choose a PDF file."

1. The file is something else, such as a Word document or a photo.
2. In Word, Google Docs or Pages, use **Save as** or **Download** and choose **PDF**. On a phone, most apps have **Print**, then **Save as PDF** or share as PDF.
3. Upload the new PDF.

## "This file is larger than 4 MB."

4. Large PDFs are usually scans or contain big photos.
5. Shrink it with a PDF compressor, or scan again at a lower resolution: 150 dpi in black and white is plenty for signing.
6. If it's a long document, split off only the pages that need signatures.
7. Upload the smaller file.

## The page shows an error instead of the document

8. Some PDFs are damaged or password-protected. Open the file on your computer; if it asks for a password, save an unprotected copy and upload that.
9. Still stuck? Re-export the PDF from the program that made it, or print it to a new PDF, and upload again.
