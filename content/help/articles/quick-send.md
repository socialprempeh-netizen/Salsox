Quick Send is for the everyday case: one PDF, and one or more people who just need to sign it. You don't place any fields yourself. We add a signature and a date for each signer at the end of the document.

## Send with Quick Send

1. In the dashboard, select **Quick Send**.
2. Choose your PDF (up to 4 MB).
3. In **Who needs to sign?**, type each signer's email address. Separate several addresses with commas or new lines.
4. Optionally open **Title, message and expiry** to name the document, add a message to the signers and choose when the links expire.
5. Select **Send to 1 signer** (or **Send to 3 signers**, and so on). Everyone gets an email with their own signing link at the same time.

You're taken to the document's page, where you can follow who has opened and signed it.

## Quick Send or the full editor?

Use **Quick Send** when every signer just signs and dates.

Use **New document** ([Send a document step by step](/resources/help/send-a-document-step-by-step)) when you need any of these:

- fields in particular places, such as initials on every page or a text box to fill in;
- people who approve, view or only get a copy instead of signing;
- a signing order, a payment with Sign & Pay, or a draft to finish later.
