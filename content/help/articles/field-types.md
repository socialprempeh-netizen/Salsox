There are seven field types. Each one asks the signer for one thing, and the signing page explains it to them as they reach it.

| Field | What the signer does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Signature** | Draws, types or uploads a signature. | The signature line. |
| **Initials** | Draws, types or uploads their initials. | Initialling each page or a changed clause. |
| **Name** | Types their full name. | A printed name under the signature. |
| **Date** | Nothing: today's date is filled in for them. | The date next to a signature. |
| **Email** | Types their email address. | Contact details on a form. |
| **Text** | Types the requested information. | An address, an ID number, an answer. |
| **Checkbox** | Ticks it, or leaves it unticked. | "I agree" boxes and options. |

## Choose and place one

1. On the **Fields** step, select the person the field is for.
2. Select the field type from the toolbar.
3. Tap the page where it goes. It appears at a size that suits its type; drag its corner to change that.
4. Decide whether it's **Required**. New fields are required, except checkboxes, which start optional so a signer can leave them unticked.

## Good to know

- A signer can't finish until every required field is filled in.
- Once someone has drawn or typed their signature, the signing page offers it again for their other signature fields, so they don't redo it.
- Fields are drawn onto the signed copy exactly where you placed them. The original PDF is never changed.
