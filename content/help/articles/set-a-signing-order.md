By default everyone is invited at the same time and signs in any order. With a signing order, each step is invited only once the step before it has finished. Signing order is part of the Business plan.

## Turn on signing order

1. On the editor's **Recipients** step, add everyone first.
2. Tick **Sign in order (one after another)** below the list.
3. Each card now shows its step, such as **Step 1**. Use the up and down arrows on a card to move that person earlier or later.
4. To let someone sign at the same time as the person above them, tick **Sign at the same time as step 2** on their card (the number is the step above).
5. Check the **Signing order** preview under the list, then continue to **Fields**.

## After sending

The document's page shows a **Signing order** box: each step is marked **Done**, **Their turn** or **Waiting**, with a line saying who signs next. Each person's card also shows their step.

## Good to know

- People who **view** or **get a copy** are never part of the order.
- Someone who opens their link before their turn sees **Not your turn yet**, and is emailed when it is.
- Keep sequences short: each step waits for the one before it.
