Your documents are yours. You can download them one at a time or all at once, on any plan, including after you cancel.

## Download one document

1. Open the document from **Documents**.
2. Under **Files**, select **Original PDF** for the file you uploaded.
3. Once everyone has signed, select **Signed PDF + certificate** (or **Signed PDF**) for the sealed, signed copy.

## Export everything

4. In the dashboard, select **Billing**.
5. Under **Export all documents**, select **Download ZIP**.
6. The ZIP holds every original, every signed PDF and every audit trail. A large account is split into several ZIP files, oldest documents first: download each **Part**.
