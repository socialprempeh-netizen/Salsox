---
title: "A signing link has expired"
description: "Renew expired signing links in one click without rebuilding the document; anything already signed is kept."
category: troubleshooting
order: 2
---

Signing links stop working after the expiry you chose when sending (30 days unless you changed it). A signer who opens an expired link sees **This link has expired**, with your name and email so they can ask you to renew it.

## Renew the links

1. Open the document from **Documents**. Its status shows **Expired**, with a note that the links expired before everyone signed.
2. Select **Renew and resend**.
3. Everyone who still has to sign gets a new email with a working link. You'll see **Links renewed and resent.**

## Good to know

- Nothing needs to be rebuilt, and anything already signed or filled in is kept.
- To avoid it next time, select **Extend links** on a document that's still waiting, before the links run out. You can also choose a longer expiry, or **Never**, on the **Send** step.
