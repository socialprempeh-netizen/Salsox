An account is free and takes about a minute. You can sign up on a phone or a computer.

## Sign up

1. Go to the [sign-up page](/signup).
2. Choose **Continue with Google** to use your Google account, or fill in your name (optional), your email address and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Create account**.
4. If you signed up with an email address, open the confirmation email we send you and select the link in it. It confirms your address and signs you in.

You land on your dashboard, ready to send your first document.

## Good to know

- **Nothing in your inbox?** Check your spam folder.
- **You can prepare documents straight away.** Uploading and setting up a document works before you confirm your email; sending unlocks once you have. See [Confirm your email address](/resources/help/confirm-your-email).
- **A free account sends 3 documents a month.** See [The free plan's monthly allowance](/resources/help/free-document-allowance).
