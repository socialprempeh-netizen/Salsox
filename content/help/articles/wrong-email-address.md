You don't need to cancel and start again. You can correct the address on the document itself.

## Correct the address

1. Open the document from **Documents**.
2. Under **Recipients**, find the person and select **Edit**.
3. Fix the **Email** (and the **Name** or **Phone** if needed).
4. Select **Save changes**.

The old link stops working straight away, and the corrected address gets an email with a fresh link. Their fields stay exactly where you placed them, and the audit trail records **Recipient details corrected**.

## Good to know

- You can edit someone until they have signed.
- If the new email can't be sent, you'll be told; select **Resend** to try again, or [share the link](/resources/help/share-a-signing-link) yourself.
