---
title: "A field won't place on the page"
description: "What to check when tapping the page places nothing, or sending stops at \"has no fields yet\", and how to send us a diagnostic."
category: troubleshooting
order: 5
---

Sending needs every signer to have at least one field. If you try to send without one, you'll see a message such as **Kofi Boateng has no fields yet. Place at least one.**, and the editor goes back to **Fields**.

## Place the field

1. On the **Fields** step, check the right person is selected at the top of the toolbar. Fields are placed for whoever is selected.
2. Select the field type, such as **Signature**. The hint should change to **Now tap the page where the field should go.**
3. Tap or click on the page itself, below the toolbar. The toolbar stays at the top as you scroll, so tap a part of the page you can see clearly.
4. The field appears where you tapped, in that person's colour, and the hint changes back.

## Still nothing appears?

5. Let the page finish loading: wait until you can read the document, then try again.
6. Reload the page. Your draft keeps everything you've saved; select **Save draft** on the **Send** step first if you've made changes.
7. If it still won't place, add `?placementDebug=1` to the end of the editor's address in your browser and press Enter. A **Placement diagnostics** box appears under the toolbar.
8. Select a field type, tap the page once, and take a screenshot of the box. Send it to us through the [contact page](/contact): it shows exactly where your tap went, so we can fix the cause.
