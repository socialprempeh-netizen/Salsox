Your profile and sign-in details are under **Settings** in the dashboard.

## Change your name

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Profile**, change your display name and save.

## Change your email address

3. Under **Email address**, type the new address and select **Change email**.
4. Confirm the change from the email sent to your current address, then from the one sent to the new address. The account moves only after both, so a typo can't lock you out.

## Set or change your password

5. Under **Sign-in methods**, type your current password and a new one (at least 8 characters), then select **Change password**.
6. If you signed up with Google and have no password yet, type one and select **Set password**. You can then sign in either way.

## Good to know

- Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?** and follow the emailed link. It works for 30 minutes.
- For extra protection, [turn on two-factor authentication](/resources/help/two-factor-authentication).
