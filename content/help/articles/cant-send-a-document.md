When a document can't be sent, a message says why. Find yours below.

## "Confirm your email address before sending."

1. Open the confirmation email we sent when you signed up, and select its link. You can ask for a new one from the **Documents** page. See [Confirm your email address](/resources/help/confirm-your-email).
2. Go back to your document and send it. It was kept while you confirmed.

## "You've sent your 3 free documents this month."

3. Your free allowance is used up until the first of next month. See [The free plan's monthly allowance](/resources/help/free-document-allowance).
4. To send now, select **Upgrade** and choose a plan.

## "… is part of the Business plan."

5. The document uses a Business feature: **Signing in order**, an **approver**, or **Sign & Pay**.
6. Either [upgrade to Business](/resources/help/plans-and-what-they-include), or switch the feature off: untick **Sign in order (one after another)**, change approvers to **Signs**, or untick **Collect a payment (Sign & Pay)**.

## "… has no fields yet. Place at least one."

7. A signer has no field. Go to **Fields**, select them and place at least one. See [A field won't place on the page](/resources/help/field-wont-place).

## "Every recipient needs a name and a valid email…"

8. On **Recipients**, check every card has a name and a complete email address, and that at least one person signs or approves.

## "You've reached today's sending limit."

9. There's a daily cap on sending, to stop spam. It resets over the next 24 hours. If you genuinely need to send more, [contact us](/contact).
