The editor gives you full control over who does what and where. It has three steps, shown at the top: **Recipients**, **Fields** and **Send**.

## Step 1: Recipients

1. [Upload your PDF](/resources/help/upload-your-first-pdf) and select **Continue to recipients**.
2. Fill in the first person's **Name** and **Email**. A **Phone** number is optional; it lets you share their link by WhatsApp or SMS later.
3. Choose their **Role**: **Signs**, **Approves**, **Views** or **Gets a copy**. See [Add recipients and choose their roles](/resources/help/add-recipients-and-roles).
4. Select **Add recipient** for each extra person (up to 10).
5. Select **Next**.

## Step 2: Fields

6. Pick a person at the top of the toolbar, then a field type such as **Signature**, then tap the page where it should go. Repeat for each field. [Place fields on a document](/resources/help/place-fields) explains moving, resizing and removing them.
7. Every signer needs at least one field. Select **Next** when you're done.

## Step 3: Send

8. Check the **Document title**. Optionally add an **Email subject** and a **Message to recipients**.
9. Choose when **Signing links expire after**: 7, 14, 30 or 90 days, or never.
10. Select **Send for signature**. Or select **Save draft** to finish later.

Each person gets an email with their own link. You're taken to the document's page to [track who has signed](/resources/help/track-a-document).
