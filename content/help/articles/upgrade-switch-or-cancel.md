Everything about your plan is on the **Billing** page in the dashboard.

## Upgrade

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Under **Available plans**, select **Upgrade to** and the plan you want, then pay on the checkout page.

## Switch plans or update your card

3. On **Billing**, select **Manage Billing**. The billing portal opens.
4. Switch between plans or between monthly and yearly billing, or update your payment method, then return to the dashboard.

## Cancel

5. On **Billing**, select **Cancel subscription**.
6. Confirm with **Yes, cancel**. You keep your plan until the date shown, and you won't be charged again.
7. Changed your mind before that date? Select **Undo cancellation**.

## Invoices and receipts

8. Every payment is listed under **Payments and receipts** on **Billing**, with a PDF receipt for each: subscription invoices, one-time purchases and Sign & Pay payments you received.

## Good to know

- We email you before a subscription renews. There's never a silent charge.
- After cancelling, your documents stay available to download, and you can [export everything](/resources/help/export-your-documents) at any time.
