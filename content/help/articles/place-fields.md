Fields are the places where each person signs, dates or fills something in. You place them on the **Fields** step of the editor. It works the same with a mouse or a finger.

## Place a field

1. Open the document in the editor and go to **Fields** (select **Next** on the Recipients step, or select **Fields** at the top).
2. In the toolbar, select the person the field is for. Each person has their own colour.
3. Select a field type: **Signature**, **Initials**, **Name**, **Date**, **Email**, **Text** or **Checkbox**. The hint under the toolbar changes to **Now tap the page where the field should go.**
4. Tap or click the page at that spot. The field appears there, in that person's colour.
5. Repeat for every field. Scroll down to reach later pages.

## Adjust a field

6. **Move it:** drag the field.
7. **Resize it:** drag the small square in its bottom-right corner.
8. **Make it optional or required:** select the field, then tick or untick **Required** in the toolbar. Required fields show a **\***.
9. **Remove it:** select the field, then select **Remove**. On a computer you can also press Delete.
10. Select **Done** in the toolbar to deselect the field.

When every signer has at least one field, select **Next** to review and send.

## Good to know

- Changed your mind about a field type? Select the same type again to cancel before tapping the page.
- Tapping a field you've already placed selects it rather than placing a new one.
- If tapping the page places nothing, see [A field won't place on the page](/resources/help/field-wont-place).
