With Sign & Pay, one signer pays an amount you set at the end of signing, by card or mobile money. The signature and the payment arrive together. Sign & Pay is part of the Business plan.

## Before you start

1. [Connect a payout account](/resources/help/connect-payouts) on **Payouts**. Without one, the editor asks you to connect one first.

## Add a payment to a document

2. Prepare the document in the editor as usual and go to the **Send** step.
3. Tick **Collect a payment (Sign & Pay)**.
4. Type the **Amount**, for example 250.00, and choose the **Currency**.
5. Under **Who pays**, choose the signer who pays.
6. Select **Send for signature**.

## What happens next

7. The payer signs as usual. The last button reads **Pay** and the amount, followed by **and finish**. They pay securely, by card or, through Paystack, mobile money.
8. On the document's page, the payer's card shows **Pays** and the amount, then **Paid** once it has gone through. The audit trail records the payment.
9. A receipt for every payment you receive is listed under **Billing**, in **Payments and receipts**.

## Good to know

- Stripe's or Paystack's processing fee comes out of what you receive, at cost. We take no cut.
- Only one signer pays per document.
