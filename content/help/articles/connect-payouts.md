Before you can collect a payment with Sign & Pay, connect a payout account. Payments go straight from your signer to that account.

## Connect Stripe

1. In the dashboard, select **Payouts**.
2. Under Stripe, choose the **Country of your business or bank account**. This can't be changed later.
3. Select **Connect Stripe**. You finish setting up on Stripe's secure page: business details, identity and bank account.
4. When you're sent back, the account shows **Ready**. If it says **Setup incomplete**, select **Continue Stripe setup** to finish, then **Refresh status**.

## Connect Paystack

1. On **Payouts**, under Paystack, type your **Business or account name**.
2. Choose your **Bank** and type your **Account number**.
3. Select **Connect Paystack**. It shows **Paystack connected.** when it's done.

## Which one?

- **Stripe** takes cards in 40+ countries.
- **Paystack** takes cards and mobile money in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa.
- Connect both if your clients are in both places. You can then [collect a payment](/resources/help/collect-a-payment) in any supported currency.
