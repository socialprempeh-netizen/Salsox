With two-factor authentication on, signing in with your password also asks for a six-digit code from an app on your phone. Someone who learns your password still can't get in.

## Turn it on

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.
2. In the dashboard, go to **Settings** and find **Two-factor authentication**.
3. Select **Turn on** and type **Your account password**.
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you can't scan it, add an account in the app by entering the key shown instead.
5. Type the **Code from the app** and select **Confirm**.
6. Save your **Backup codes** somewhere safe. They're shown once, and each one signs you in if you lose your phone.

## Turn it off

7. In **Settings**, under **Two-factor authentication**, select **Turn off** and confirm. Turning it on again later pairs a new authenticator from scratch.

## Good to know

- Accounts with two-factor authentication sign in with their password and a code, not with an emailed sign-in link.
- Lost the backup codes? While you're signed in, select **New backup codes** to make a new set; the old ones stop working.
