## At a glance

Prices below are what each company publishes for its e-signature product. DocuSign's figures were taken from its pricing page on **6 October 2026** and are listed there in USD, billed annually. Prices change; check the source before you decide.

| | {site} | DocuSign |
|---|---|---|
| Entry paid plan | Personal: $9/month or $90/year | Personal: $132/year (shown as $11/month, billed annually) |
| Sending on the entry plan | No envelope count (fair use) | 5 envelopes per month |
| Team plans | No team seats today | Standard: $360 per user per year, 100 envelopes per user per year |
| Plan with payment collection | Business: $19/month or $190/year | Business Pro: $540 per user per year |
| Bulk send | Not offered | Business Pro and above |
| Free option | 3 documents a month, plus free PDF tools | Free trial |
| Pricing basis | Per account | Per user (seat) |

List prices are a starting point, not the whole bill. DocuSign sells add-ons and negotiates larger contracts, and taxes vary by country; {site} adds nothing beyond the plan price, though payment providers charge their own fees on Sign & Pay transactions.

## Where DocuSign is stronger

DocuSign has been building e-signature software for two decades, and its breadth shows. It offers reusable templates, bulk sending, conditional routing (listed under its Enhanced plans), a public API for starting signing from other systems, a large catalogue of integrations, and administration features for organisations with many senders. If your signing happens inside a CRM, at volume, or under an IT department's controls, those features matter, and {site} does not have them.

## Where {site} is different

**How sending is counted.** DocuSign's published plans set envelope allowances: 5 a month on Personal, 100 per user per year on Standard and Business Pro. {site}'s paid plans do not count envelopes at all. If your volume varies, or you would rather not track it, that removes a recurring decision.

**What payment collection costs.** On DocuSign's list, collecting payment during signing starts at Business Pro, $540 per user per year. {site} includes Sign & Pay on Business at $190 a year, with Stripe for cards and Paystack for card and mobile money payments across several African markets.

**Fixing mistakes.** In {site}, a mistyped recipient address is corrected in place and an expired document is renewed with one click, keeping the signatures already given.

**Reaching signers.** Any {site} signing link can be shared over WhatsApp or SMS from the document page, alongside email, without an add-on. DocuSign lists SMS delivery as an add-on charged per delivery, with a small one-time allowance on Standard.

**Leaving.** {site} exports every original, signed PDF and audit trail in one ZIP on every plan, including after you cancel.

## Which should you choose?

Choose DocuSign if you need templates, bulk sending, integrations or an API today, or if your organisation already standardises on it. Choose {site} if you are an individual or a small business that sends its own contracts, wants signers to finish on their phones, wants to take a deposit with the signature, and would rather pay a flat price than count envelopes.
