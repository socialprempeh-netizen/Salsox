## Two different products

**GetAccept is built around the sales deal.** Its home page calls it an "AI Digital Sales Room", and it is aimed at revenue teams in companies of every size. A seller builds a proposal in GetAccept's editor, shares it with the buyer in a room alongside other material, sees how the buyer engages with it, and collects the signature at the end. The value is in the process before the signature: content, tracking, CRM data and collaboration with the buyer.

**{site} starts where the document is finished.** You already have the PDF, whether a quote, a lease, a consent form or a contract, and you need the other person to sign it, and often pay, from their phone. {site} does that last step and nothing before it.

## Where GetAccept is stronger

For a sales team, GetAccept does far more. Its plans include an in-app editor, templates and a content library, engagement video, automated reminders and chat with the buyer. Professional adds AI writing and meeting summaries, mutual action plans, pricing tables, team management, eID verification and CRM integrations. Enterprise adds CPQ, single sign-on and API access. If your contracts come out of a sales pipeline and several people work each deal, none of that exists in {site}.

## Where {site} is different

**Small commitment.** {site} has a free plan, monthly billing and a flat price per account. There is no per-user pricing, no seat minimum and no annual agreement to sign before you can send.

**Signing on any phone.** {site} signers open a link in their browser and fill one field at a time; links can be shared by WhatsApp or SMS as well as email.

**Getting paid at signing.** Sign & Pay is part of the Business plan, not an add-on: the signer pays a deposit or fee by card through Stripe, or by card and mobile money through Paystack, before the document completes.

**Fixable sends.** A wrong email address or an expired link is corrected on the same document, keeping everything already signed.

## Which should you choose?

If you run a sales team that builds proposals, works deals in a CRM and wants to see how buyers engage before they sign, GetAccept is designed for that job and {site} is not. If you are a freelancer, landlord, clinic or small business with documents already written, and you need them signed, and perhaps paid, without asking anyone to install anything or committing to a year of seats, {site} fits better and costs less to start.
