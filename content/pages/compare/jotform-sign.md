## At a glance

Jotform's figures were taken from its pricing and Jotform Sign pages on **15 October 2026**, in US dollars. Jotform Sign is included in Jotform's plans, whose limits cover forms, submissions and signed documents together. Prices change; check the sources before you decide.

| First paid plan | Personal: $9/month or $90/year, no document count | Bronze: $39/month, or $34/month billed yearly, 100 signed documents a month |
| Higher plans | Business: $19/month, adds Sign & Pay, signing order, sealed PDFs | Silver $49 ($39 yearly), 250 signed documents; Gold $129 ($99 yearly), 1,000 |

## Where Jotform is stronger

Jotform's strength is that signing sits inside a complete form platform. You can build a form, collect answers and payments, and turn PDFs into e-sign documents in the same account, choosing from a large template library, sending in bulk and routing documents through approval flows. Its free plan also signs more documents a month than {site}'s. If your work starts with forms (registrations, applications, surveys), Jotform keeps everything in one place.

## Where {site} is different

**No per-document ceiling.** {site}'s paid plans do not count documents. Jotform's plans set a monthly signed-document limit, so heavy senders move up the tiers.

**Lower price for a single sender.** {site} Personal costs less than Jotform's first paid plan.

**Paid as they sign.** {site} Business takes the payment inside the signing flow, by card through Stripe or by card and mobile money through Paystack.

**Phone-first signing without an app.** Signers open a link in any browser and sign one field at a time; links can be shared to WhatsApp or SMS.

**Fixes in place.** Wrong emails and expired links are corrected on the same document.

## Switching between them

If you move from Jotform Sign to {site}, download your signed documents and their audit trails from Jotform first; they remain valid records. Your Jotform forms and their submissions stay in Jotform, and {site} has no form builder to replace them, so many teams keep Jotform for forms and use a separate tool only for signatures. Jotform Sign templates do not carry over; start from one of {site}'s ready-made templates or upload the PDF. If you collect payments through Jotform forms today, {site}'s Sign & Pay on the Business plan can take that payment inside the signing step instead, by card or mobile money.

## Questions to ask before choosing

How many documents do you send in a typical month, and how many in your busiest? Do the people who sign usually also fill in a form first? Do you need the signer to pay at the same time? The answers point clearly to one or the other.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Jotform if you already use its forms, need its templates and approval flows, or send fewer than ten documents a month for free. Choose {site} if you send many documents and want a flat price, or need deposits collected as part of signing, particularly by mobile money.
