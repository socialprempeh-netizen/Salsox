## At a glance

SignWell's figures were taken from its pricing page on **15 October 2026**, in US dollars. SignWell shows a yearly and a monthly price for each plan; both are given. Prices change; check the source before you decide.

| | {site} | SignWell |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | 3 documents a month | 3 documents a month, 1 sender, 1 template |
| Entry paid plan | Personal: $9/month or $90/year | Light: $10/month billed yearly, $12 monthly; 1 sender, 5 templates per sender |
| Small team plan | No team seats today | Business: $30/month billed yearly, $36 monthly; 3 senders, unlimited templates |
| Pricing basis | Per account | Per sender |
| Documents on paid plans | No count (fair use) | Unlimited |
| Templates | Ready-made library, customisable and savable on Personal; not from your own documents | On every plan, up to the plan's limit |
| Bulk send | Not offered | Listed |
| API | Webhooks only; no sending API | 25 API documents a month free, then per document |
| Payment with signature | Business plan (Stripe or Paystack) | Not listed on the pricing page |
| Audit trail | Every plan; sealed PDF with certificate on Business | Listed as audit trail and tamper-proof documents |

## Where SignWell is stronger

SignWell gives more to people who send the same documents repeatedly: templates on every plan, unlimited on Business, plus bulk sending and template links. Its API includes a free monthly allowance, useful for a small developer integration. Its Business plan includes three senders, which suits a small team where several people send under one account with their own logins. If templates or an API are central to how you work, SignWell covers them and {site} does not.

## Where {site} is different

**Getting paid at signing.** {site}'s Business plan includes Sign & Pay: a deposit or fee is paid by card through Stripe, or by card and mobile money through Paystack, as part of signing. Payment collection is not listed on SignWell's pricing page.

**Signing order and approvers.** {site} Business supports sequential signing and an approver role for internal sign-off before a client sees a document.

**WhatsApp and SMS.** Every {site} signing link can be shared to WhatsApp or SMS from the document page.

**Fixes in place.** A wrong email or an expired link is corrected or renewed on the same document.

**Free tools.** The free browser tools sign and fill PDFs without an account or upload.

## Switching between them

Download your completed documents and their audit trails before closing either account; they stay valid records, and nothing needs to be signed again. SignWell templates do not carry over to {site}, which does not import other providers' templates: for each document, start from one of {site}'s ready-made templates or upload the PDF and place fields in the editor. If you used SignWell's API, that integration has no equivalent in {site}. Try one of your regular documents in both before deciding, and time how long setup takes.

## Which should you choose?

Choose SignWell if templates, bulk sending or an API save you real time. Choose {site} if you want to collect deposits as clients sign, especially by mobile money, or need a signing order with approvals. The free plans are close enough that the quickest way to decide is to send one real document through each.
