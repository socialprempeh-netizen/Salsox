Prices change; check the sources before you decide.

Adobe's monthly-billed annual plans carry a cancellation fee of up to half the remaining commitment after 14 days, according to its pricing page. {site} plans can be cancelled at any time.

## Where Adobe is stronger

If your organisation already lives in Acrobat, its e-signatures are simply there: the same software that edits, converts and combines PDFs can send them for signature. Acrobat Pro adds reusable templates and bulk sending, and Adobe's standalone Acrobat Sign adds signing inside Microsoft Word and Teams, Salesforce and Workday, web forms that visitors fill and sign on your site, and the compliance features large organisations expect. If you need PDF editing, enterprise integrations or an IT-managed rollout, Adobe is the more complete choice and {site} is not trying to compete with that.

## Where {site} is different

**Price for small senders.** A single sender pays less on {site} than for the cheapest Acrobat plan, and {site} is priced per account rather than per license, with no annual commitment.

**Signing on phones.** Signers open a link in any phone browser, see one field at a time, and need no account or app. Links can be shared by WhatsApp or SMS as well as email, which matters where clients read messages before email.

**Payments in African markets.** Sign & Pay works with Paystack as well as Stripe, so a signer can pay a deposit by mobile money where Paystack supports it.

**Fixes in place.** A wrong email address or an expired link is corrected or renewed on the same document, without starting over.

**Free tools.** The free browser tools sign, fill and add signatures to PDFs without an account or upload.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Adobe if you need a PDF editor as well as signatures, if your team already pays for Acrobat, or if signing must live inside Microsoft, Salesforce or Workday. Choose {site} if you only need documents signed, and often paid, by people on their phones, and you would rather pay a small flat price than a license per person.
