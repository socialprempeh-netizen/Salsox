## What an online eSignature is

An online eSignature is a way of agreeing to a document that happens on a screen instead of on paper. You prepare the document once as a PDF, mark where each person signs, and send it. Each signer opens a private link, reviews the pages, and signs by drawing with a finger, typing their name or uploading an image of their signature. When the last person has signed, everyone gets the finished PDF.

The signature itself is the visible part. What makes it useful as evidence is everything recorded around it: the email address the link was sent to, when it was opened, when each field was filled, the IP address and browser of the signer, and a fingerprint of the final file. {site} keeps that record as an append-only audit trail, and on the Business plan prints it on a certificate page at the end of the signed PDF.

## Why signing from a phone matters

Most people who receive a contract today read it on a phone, often between other things. A signing page built for a desktop, with tiny fields to pinch and zoom into, is where agreements stall. {site} walks the signer through one field at a time with a large button to move on, keeps the page readable at phone width, and does not ask them to create an account first. The link can also be shared over WhatsApp or SMS from the document's page, which is how many signers prefer to be reached.

## How it works

1. **Upload the PDF.** Any PDF up to 4 MB. The original is stored as it is and never modified.
2. **Add who signs.** Up to ten recipients per document, as signers, approvers, viewers or people copied on the result.
3. **Place the fields.** Signature, initials, name, date, text and checkboxes, or let Quick Send place a signature and date for each signer automatically.
4. **Send.** Each signer gets an email with their link, and you can share the same link by WhatsApp or SMS. Reminders go out every three days until they sign.
5. **Download the signed copy.** When everyone has signed, the sealed PDF is stored, emailed to all parties and available to download or export.

## When an online eSignature is the right tool

Online signing suits the agreements most people and small businesses deal with: client contracts, service agreements, consent and onboarding forms, quotes, leases, offer letters and NDAs. It removes printing, scanning and couriers, and it gives you a record you can show later.

It is not the right tool for documents your jurisdiction requires to be signed in ink or witnessed in person, such as wills and some property deeds in many countries. For those, the law, not the software, decides. The guide to whether [electronic signatures are legal](/blog/are-electronic-signatures-legal) covers the main frameworks and their exceptions.
