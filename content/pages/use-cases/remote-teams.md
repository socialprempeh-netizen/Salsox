## The problem with paper at a distance

A distributed team runs into paper at the worst moments: a new hire in another city who has no printer, a director travelling when a supplier contract needs a countersignature, a contractor three time zones away whose agreement must be signed before they can start. Each one turns a five-minute task into days of scanning, emailing and waiting.

## Everything signed from a link

With {site}, each document goes out as a link. The recipient opens it on whatever they have, usually a phone, and signs one field at a time, with no account or app to install. Links can be sent by email, or shared by WhatsApp or SMS from the document's page, which helps when a colleague is on the road and not checking email.

## Time zones without the chasing

Nobody needs to be online at the same time. The sender sends at the end of their day; the signer signs at the start of theirs. Each document's page shows who has opened and signed, and anyone who has not signed is reminded automatically every three days. When the last person signs, everyone receives the completed copy. See [document tracking](/document-tracking) for how status and reminders work.

## Approvals across the team

Many remote teams need an internal sign-off before a document goes out: a manager approving an offer, a founder approving a large contract. On the Business plan, an approver can be placed ahead of the external signer, and signing set to sequential, so the document only reaches the client once the approval is in. The [signing workflows page](/document-workflows) explains the options.

## The documents remote teams sign most

- **Hiring**: offer letters and employment contracts, which candidates sign from their phones; the [job offer letter](/templates/job-offer-letter) and [employment contract](/templates/employment-contract) templates are a starting point.
- **Contractors**: agreements with freelancers and contractors in other countries, often with an NDA first.
- **Clients**: proposals, statements of work and service agreements, signed and, with Sign & Pay on the Business plan, paid in one step.
- **Policies**: remote-working terms and data-security rules that each person should acknowledge; the [remote work agreement](/templates/remote-work-agreement) template covers schedule, availability, equipment and data security.

## Security and records

Every document has an audit trail of each view and signature, with time, IP address and device, which matters more when nobody saw the other person sign. Accounts can use two-factor authentication. Signed documents and their trails can be exported at any time.

## What it does not do

{site} has no team seats or shared admin console: each account is one sender. For a remote team where a few people send documents, that is often enough; for a large organisation where many people send under central administration, a platform with team management fits better.
