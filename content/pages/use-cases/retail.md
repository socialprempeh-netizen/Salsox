## The paperwork behind the counter

A shop's busiest paperwork happens away from customers. Stock comes in under supplier terms and orders. The premises are held on a lease that will one day be renewed. Staff are hired, often seasonally, and each needs an offer and a contract. Deposits are taken on special orders. Every one of these needs a signature, and the owner who should sign is usually on the shop floor.

## Suppliers and orders

Regular stock is easier to manage under a [supplier agreement](/templates/supplier-agreement) that fixes prices, delivery and returns, with a [purchase order](/templates/purchase-order) for each delivery. The supplier accepts the order by signing, and the PO number on their invoice makes it easy to check what arrived against what was ordered and what was billed.

## The premises

Shops, kiosks and small units are let on commercial terms, which differ from a home lease: permitted use, service charges, fit-out, signage and a break clause. The [commercial lease agreement](/templates/commercial-lease-agreement) covers them, with the tenant's initials on the repairs and break clauses. When a lease is due for renewal, a short renewal agreement records the new term and rent without redrafting everything.

## Seasonal and permanent staff

Retail hiring peaks before holidays, and candidates compare offers quickly. The [job offer letter](/templates/job-offer-letter) can be accepted from a phone the same day, followed by the [employment contract](/templates/employment-contract). A [handbook acknowledgment](/templates/employee-handbook-acknowledgment) records that each person received the policies on cash handling, lateness and conduct, which matters the first time one of them is broken.

## Special orders and deposits

For made-to-order goods, a [deposit invoice](/templates/deposit-invoice) sets the total, the deposit and whether it can be refunded. On the Business plan, the customer signs and pays the deposit in one step, by card or by mobile money through Paystack, so the order is confirmed and funded before you place it with your supplier.

## Events and promotions

Store openings and community events sometimes bring sponsors or partner brands. A [sponsorship agreement](/templates/sponsorship-agreement) lists exactly what each partner receives, so the banner, the stand and the social posts are agreed in writing.

## Franchises and concessions

Some retailers run concessions inside other stores, or franchise a second branch. These relationships rest on agreements that set fees, standards and territory; adapt the [service agreement](/templates/service-agreement) or a [partnership agreement](/templates/partnership-agreement) as a starting point and take advice on franchise rules, which are regulated in many countries. Once the terms are agreed, both sides sign from their phones and keep the same signed copy.

## On a phone, between customers

Everything above can be sent and signed from a phone. Links go out by email, WhatsApp or SMS; signers need no account; the documents list shows what is still waiting; and reminders follow up on their own. Each signed document comes back with an audit trail of who signed and when.
