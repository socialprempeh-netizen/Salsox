## When this tool is the right one

Use it when you are the person signing and you just need a signed file to send back: a form a landlord emailed you, a consent letter, a quote to accept, an agreement a client sent as a PDF. You sign your own copy and return it the way it came.

If you are the one who needs signatures from other people, this is not the tool. A signed file you produced yourself records nothing about who signed it or when; for that, [request a signature](/request-signature) through a signing workflow, which keeps an audit trail and gives everyone the final copy.

## What it does to your file

The tool adds your signature and any date or text you place as part of the page content, then saves a new PDF. If the document contains fillable form fields, they are flattened so the signed copy cannot be quietly edited in a PDF reader afterwards. Your original file on your device is not changed; the signed copy downloads under a new name ending in "(signed)".

Signatures drawn on screen are smoothed and saved as a transparent image, so they sit naturally on the page whatever its background. Typed signatures are set in a script typeface. Uploaded images are scaled down before use, which keeps the signed PDF small enough to email.

## Why it does not upload anything

Most online PDF signing sites upload your document to their servers, process it there and send it back. That means a copy of your contract exists, at least for a while, on a machine you know nothing about. This tool uses the same PDF engine {site} uses to seal signed documents, but runs it inside your browser. The privacy benefit is not a policy; it is how the tool is built.

## Tips for a clean result

- Place the signature over the signature line, then make it a little smaller than the box: a signature that overlaps text looks careless when printed.
- Add the date with the date mark rather than typing it by hand, so it is aligned and legible.
- For initials on every page, use [Add signature to PDF](/add-signature-to-pdf), which can repeat a mark on each page in one step.
- If the PDF has boxes to fill in, [Fill and sign PDF](/fill-and-sign-pdf) fills the form's own fields first.
