## How this differs from signing a PDF

The [Sign PDF](/sign-pdf) tool is built around one act: you sign a document once, with the date, and download it. This tool is built around **placement**. It is for when you already have a signature image you want to reuse, when a document needs the same mark in several places, or when every page needs your initials.

Contracts, especially tenancy agreements, loan documents and long service agreements, often ask the signer to initial each page. Doing that by hand in most PDF apps means placing the same image again and again. Here you place your initials once on the first page, turn on "every page", and the download has them at the same spot on every page.

## Getting a good signature image

The quality of the result depends mostly on the image you start with.

- **Transparent PNG is best.** The signature sits on the page with nothing behind it, whatever the paper colour. The [PDF signature generator](/pdf-signature-generator) makes one from a drawn or typed signature in a few seconds.
- **Crop tightly.** Empty space around the signature makes it harder to place and shrinks the visible ink when it is scaled to fit a box.
- **Use dark ink.** Black or dark blue stays readable when the PDF is printed in greyscale.
- **Keep it modest in size.** The tool scales large images down before using them, which keeps the signed PDF small enough to email.

## What happens to the PDF

The tool reads your PDF, draws each placed image onto its page at the position you chose, flattens any fillable form fields so the result cannot be edited without trace, and saves a new copy whose name ends in "(signed)". Your original file is untouched. All of this happens in your browser using the same stamping engine {site} uses when it seals documents signed through the service, so a signature looks identical whichever way it was placed.

## When you need more than an image

An image on a page shows intent; it does not prove who put it there. If the other party might later ask who signed and when, collect the signature through a signing workflow instead, which records an audit trail.
